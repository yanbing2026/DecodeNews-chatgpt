# 美国中期选举前已有约5000万人提前投票，民主党在多个关键州看到有利信号

美国2026年中期选举投票日前夕，约5,000万名选民已经提前投票。The New York Times 11月2日报道，多个关键参议院战场州的提前投票人数高于上一轮中期选举同期水平，部分州登记为民主党的选民在提前投票中占比上升。相关数据为民主党带来一定乐观情绪，但提前投票统计并不能直接显示选民支持哪位候选人。

## 提前投票规模明显扩大

The New York Times 报道称，截至11月2日早间，全国已有约5,000万张选票提前投出。主要参议院竞争州的提前投票参与度普遍高于上一轮中期选举同一阶段。提前投票包括邮寄选票和提前到投票地点现场投票，反映选民参与时间安排的变化，也为竞选团队提供了观察投票热度的窗口。

## 部分州民主党登记选民比例上升

该报对多个关键州的分析显示，在部分地区，提前投票中登记为民主党的选民占比高于2024年。美国南部一些州的提前投票者中，黑人选民比例也高于通常水平。由于黑人选民整体上更倾向民主党，这些结构性变化被民主党视为有利信号，但不能据此断言这些选民都投给民主党候选人。

## 提前投票数据存在解释限制

不同州的选举规则、选民登记制度和数据公开方式并不相同。有些州会公布投票者的党派登记、年龄或族裔信息，有些州则几乎不提供这类数据。提前投票数据可以显示参与度和选民构成，却无法直接揭示选票选择。候选人也可能在正式投票日获得不同群体的支持，因此最终结果仍需等待计票。

## 民主党对参议院战场抱有期待

The New York Times 报道称，民主党被广泛认为有机会赢回众议院，同时也希望在参议院取得突破。该报对参议院关键州的分析指出，多个战场州提前投票数据为民主党提供了鼓舞，但参议院控制权仍存在不确定性。全国提前投票总量本身不能直接换算为某一党的席位优势。

## 高投票率可能改变选举格局

提前投票增加可能意味着本次选举总体参与度较高，但投票时间提前并不自动意味着某一党获胜。投票率、候选人动员、邮寄选票处理和各州计票进度都会影响结果。随着投票日到来，提前投票数据将与现场投票结果一起构成完整选举图景。当前数据最能说明的是选民参与度强劲，而不是最终胜负已经揭晓。

# About 50 Million Americans Have Voted Early Ahead of the Midterms, Offering Democrats Encouraging Signals

About 50 million Americans had already cast ballots before Election Day in the 2026 midterm elections, according to reporting by The New York Times on November 2. Early voting was higher than at the same point in the previous midterm cycle across several major Senate battleground states. In some states, registered Democrats made up a larger share of early voters than they did in 2024. The figures offered Democrats reasons for optimism, but early-voting data do not reveal how people actually voted.

## Early voting reaches a large scale

The New York Times reported that roughly 50 million ballots had been cast by the morning of November 2. Turnout was notably higher than at the same stage of the previous midterm cycle in major Senate battleground states. The early-voting total includes both mail ballots and in-person voting before Election Day.

The figures provide campaigns with an indication of participation and voter engagement before the final day of voting. They do not, by themselves, show which candidates are receiving support. The ballots will be counted under each state’s procedures, and the results will become clearer only as election officials report the vote.

## Registered Democrats make up a larger share in some states

The Times’ analysis found that the share of early ballots cast by registered Democrats had increased in several key states compared with 2024. In parts of the South, Black voters also represented a larger share of early voters than is typical. Because Black voters tend to support Democrats, those changes were viewed by Democratic strategists as encouraging signs.

However, party registration and demographic patterns are not the same as a record of individual votes. A registered Democrat may vote for a Republican, and a voter’s demographic category does not determine a ballot choice. The figures offer clues about turnout composition, not a direct count of support for either party.

## Early-voting data have important limits

States differ in their election rules and in the information they make public. Some states provide data about voters’ party registration, age or race, while others disclose very little demographic information. That makes comparisons across states difficult.

Early voting can show how many people have participated and, where data are available, which groups are turning out. It cannot reliably predict how those voters marked their ballots. Election Day participation may also change the composition of the electorate, and the final result depends on all ballots counted under state law.

## Democrats see opportunities in Senate battlegrounds

The Times reported that Democrats were widely expected to have a strong chance of winning control of the House and believed they also had an opportunity to flip the Senate. Early-voting patterns in several battleground states added to that optimism, although the Senate outcome remained uncertain.

The national total of early ballots cannot be translated directly into a projected number of seats. Senate races are decided state by state, and local candidates, turnout patterns and the remaining votes can produce different outcomes in different contests.

## High participation does not settle the election

The early-voting surge may indicate that overall participation will be high, but voting early does not automatically benefit one party. The final results will reflect early ballots, Election Day voting, the handling of mail ballots and each state’s counting and certification process.

The clearest conclusion from the available data is that participation was strong before Election Day and that some battleground states showed changes in the composition of early voters. Whether those patterns translate into Democratic victories will be known only after the votes are counted.
