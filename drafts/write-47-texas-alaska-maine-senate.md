# DDHQ预测德州、阿拉斯加和缅因州参议院选战可能转向民主党

Decision Desk HQ（DDHQ）在美国2026年中期选举前更新的参议院预测显示，民主党候选人在德州、阿拉斯加和缅因州的胜选概率上升，三场选战可能使民主党获得参议院多数。The Hill 11月2日报道，DDHQ给德州民主党候选人 James Talarico 和阿拉斯加民主党候选人 Mary Peltola 各自61%的胜选概率，并给缅因州民主党候选人 Troy Jackson 63%的胜选概率。

## 三场选战改变参院预测

根据 DDHQ 的模型，民主党有望以52比48的席位优势控制参议院。该机构估计民主党赢得参议院的整体概率为64%，共和党为36%。这些数字是选举预测，不是已经计票的结果。The Hill 同时指出，多个参议院选战仍处于民调误差范围内，统计上可能接近势均力敌。

## 德州与阿拉斯加成为关键战场

德州由民主党候选人 James Talarico 挑战共和党候选人，阿拉斯加则由 Mary Peltola 挑战现任参议员 Dan Sullivan。DDHQ给予两位民主党候选人各61%的胜选概率。若其中任何一场出现与传统党派倾向不同的结果，都可能改变参议院席位的整体计算。预测结果也受到候选人个人表现、投票率和州内政治环境影响。

## 缅因州Jackson挑战Collins

在缅因州，民主党候选人 Troy Jackson 被 DDHQ 评估为有63%的胜选概率，挑战共和党参议员 Susan Collins。The Hill 报道称，近期 The New York Times 与 Siena University 的民调显示，民主党在多个竞争激烈的参议院选区表现强劲，但大多数选战仍接近误差范围。概率模型和民调结果都不能替代正式投票。

## 民主党优势与特朗普支持率有关

The Hill 将民主党近期表现改善与特朗普支持率下降、选民对生活成本的不满联系起来。报道还提到，伊朗战争和与加拿大的关税争端推高了汽油、化肥等商品价格，尤其影响农业州选民。与此同时，民主党内部也出现不利消息：密歇根州众议员 Haley Stevens 在投票日前转而支持共和党候选人 Mike Rogers，给民主党竞选团队带来额外争议。

## 预测不等于席位已经翻转

DDHQ的“flip blue”表述指的是模型预测民主党有机会赢得原本由共和党掌握的席位，并不意味着选举结果已经确定。许多参议院选区仍可能出现紧张竞争，投票率、选民结构和最后阶段的竞选变化都可能影响结果。参议院最终控制权必须等到各州完成投票、计票和认证后才能确认。

# DDHQ Forecast Says Senate Races in Texas, Alaska and Maine Could Shift to Democrats

Decision Desk HQ has forecast that Democrats could flip Senate races in Texas, Alaska and Maine in the 2026 midterm elections, potentially putting the party in position to regain control of the chamber. The Hill reported on November 2 that the forecasting organization gave Democratic candidates James Talarico in Texas and Mary Peltola in Alaska a 61% chance of winning. It assigned Maine Democrat Troy Jackson a 63% chance of defeating Republican Senator Susan Collins.

## Three contests reshape the Senate forecast

DDHQ’s model projected that Democrats could win a 52-48 advantage in the Senate. It gave Democrats an overall 64% probability of winning control of the chamber, compared with 36% for Republicans. These are forecast probabilities, not vote counts or confirmed election outcomes.

The Hill also noted that several Senate contests remained within polling margins of error. That means the races could still be statistically close even when a forecasting model assigns one candidate a higher probability. The model’s estimate is not a guarantee that a particular candidate will win.

## Texas and Alaska emerge as key battlegrounds

In Texas, Democrat James Talarico is challenging the Republican candidate in a race that has become more competitive than many observers expected. In Alaska, Democrat Mary Peltola is challenging incumbent Republican Senator Dan Sullivan. DDHQ gave both Democratic candidates a 61% chance of victory.

If Democrats win either race, the result could alter the overall Senate balance. But the outcome will depend on turnout, candidate performance and state-specific political conditions. A forecast probability summarizes the model’s assessment of those factors; it does not establish that a seat has already changed party control.

## Jackson challenges Collins in Maine

In Maine, DDHQ gave Democrat Troy Jackson a 63% chance of defeating Republican Senator Susan Collins. The Hill reported that recent polling from The New York Times and Siena University showed Democrats performing strongly in several competitive Senate races. However, most of those contests remained close, with many polling results still within the margin of error.

The Maine race is therefore one of several contests that could influence the chamber’s future. The model’s estimate may change as new information becomes available, and the result will ultimately be determined by votes cast and counted in the state.

## Democratic gains are linked to voter concerns

The Hill connected the improved Democratic outlook to declining approval ratings for President Donald Trump and voter concerns about the cost of living. The report also cited the war involving Iran and tariff disputes with Canada as factors that had increased prices for goods such as gasoline and fertilizer, particularly affecting agricultural states.

Democrats also faced internal political challenges. The Hill reported that Michigan Representative Haley Stevens reversed her endorsement of Democratic Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed and backed Republican nominee Mike Rogers shortly before Election Day. The move created controversy within the party, although the report noted that some Democrats and analysts believed it might not change the overall direction of the race.

## Forecasts do not mean seats have already flipped

The headline description that races could “flip blue” refers to the forecast that Democrats have a chance to win seats currently held by Republicans. It does not mean that the election has been decided. Turnout, voter composition and late campaign developments can still affect the outcome.

Control of the Senate will be known only after states complete voting, counting and certification. Until then, DDHQ’s probabilities are a snapshot of the model’s assessment on November 2, not a substitute for the election results.
