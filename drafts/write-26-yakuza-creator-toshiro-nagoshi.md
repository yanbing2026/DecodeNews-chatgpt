# 《如龙》创作者名越稔洋的工作室正式解散，《Gang of Dragon》开发终止

日本官方公报确认，游戏制作人名越稔洋创立的 Nagoshi Studio 已于 2026 年 10 月 31 日正式解散。工作室尚未推出首款作品，其正在开发的犯罪题材动作游戏《Gang of Dragon》也随之失去继续制作的团队。此前，投资方 NetEase 停止为工作室提供资金，项目未能找到新的支持者。

## 公报确认公司解散

Polygon 11 月 2 日报道，Nagoshi Studio 的解散信息刊登在日本官方公报《官报》（Kanpo）上，解散日期为 10 月 31 日。PC Gamer 同日也核对了这一公告。工作室由《如龙》系列创作者名越稔洋创立，曾被视为他离开 Sega 后打造新作品的平台。公司解散意味着团队不再以原有法人实体继续开发游戏。

## 项目资金缺口难以填补

《Gang of Dragon》在 2025 年 The Game Awards 上首次公开，预告片展示了以东京歌舞伎町为背景的黑帮故事，韩国演员 Ma Dong-seok 参与主演。游戏被描述为一款带有《如龙》风格的犯罪动作作品。Polygon 援引此前 Bloomberg 报道称，NetEase 在 2026 年 3 月决定停止资助；当时项目据报还需要追加 70 亿日元，约合 4,400 万美元，才能完成。工作室随后未能找到替代投资方。

## 关闭发生在行业收缩背景下

NetEase 近年来持续调整游戏投资组合，并缩减部分海外工作室投入。Polygon 将 Nagoshi Studio 的关闭放在这一投资收缩背景下讨论。对《Gang of Dragon》而言，问题不仅是资金缺口，也在于原投资方退出后没有新的出资者接手。现有报道没有披露项目完成度、已投入总额或员工后续去向，因此这些情况不能据此推断。

## 名越的开发履历

Nagoshi Studio 于 2022 年成立，由名越及部分曾参与 Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio 工作的成员组成。名越此前在 Sega 工作超过 30 年，参与过 Daytona USA、Shenmue、Super Monkey Ball 等作品，并主导打造《如龙》系列。工作室关闭并不等于名越本人退出游戏行业，但《Gang of Dragon》作为该团队首款作品，已无法按原计划继续开发。

目前可以确认的是公司解散日期、NetEase 停止资金支持以及项目未能找到替代投资者。尚无公开资料说明游戏资产是否会转让、其他发行商是否可能接手，或开发团队是否会以新公司形式重组。除非相关各方进一步公布消息，这些问题仍应视为未知。

# Yakuza Creator Toshihiro Nagoshi’s Studio Officially Closes, Ending Gang of Dragon

Nagoshi Studio, the developer founded by Yakuza creator Toshihiro Nagoshi, has formally dissolved, bringing its work on the crime-action game Gang of Dragon to an end. Japan’s official government gazette, Kanpo, listed the company’s dissolution as taking effect on October 31, 2026. The studio had not released a game before closing. Its first project, Gang of Dragon, was unveiled in 2025 but failed to secure the financing needed to reach completion after investor NetEase withdrew its support.

## Official notice confirms dissolution

Polygon reported on November 2 that the dissolution notice appeared in Kanpo, Japan’s official gazette. PC Gamer independently described the same notice and said the studio’s website had disappeared months earlier. The legal dissolution date was October 31. The notice resolves months of uncertainty about the company’s status: its online presence had faded, but no formal announcement had immediately clarified whether the developer was still operating.

Nagoshi Studio was established by Nagoshi after his long career at Sega. The company was intended to give the veteran producer a new platform outside the publisher where he had built much of his reputation. Its closure means the developer will not release the game it had been preparing under the original corporate structure.

## Funding gap proved difficult to replace

Gang of Dragon was first shown at The Game Awards in 2025. Its cinematic reveal presented a crime story set in Tokyo’s Kabukicho district and featured Korean actor Ma Dong-seok. The project was widely compared with the Yakuza and Like a Dragon games because of its urban setting and gangster subject matter, although the available footage was not enough to establish how its gameplay would ultimately differ.

The financial problem became public earlier in 2026. Polygon cited Bloomberg’s March reporting that NetEase had decided to stop funding Nagoshi Studio after learning that the game would require another 7 billion yen, or about $44 million, to finish. The studio reportedly could not find another backer willing to cover that gap. Those figures describe the reported additional funding requirement, not the project’s total development cost.

## Closure reflects a wider investment pullback

NetEase has been reducing parts of its gaming portfolio and divesting some overseas investments. Polygon placed Nagoshi Studio’s closure within that broader retrenchment, which has also affected other externally supported development teams. The available reporting does not establish that every studio closure followed the same financial circumstances, but it does show that Nagoshi Studio was not operating in an environment of expanding publisher support.

For Gang of Dragon, the withdrawal of its principal backer created a practical obstacle: a substantial amount of additional financing was reportedly needed, and no replacement investor emerged. The studio’s dissolution now removes the original development organization as well. There has been no confirmed announcement that another publisher has acquired the project or that development will continue elsewhere.

## Nagoshi’s long career at Sega

Nagoshi Studio was founded in 2022 by Nagoshi and members of the team associated with Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio, the Sega group behind the Yakuza and Like a Dragon franchises. Nagoshi had spent more than 30 years at Sega. His work there included Daytona USA, Shenmue and Super Monkey Ball, before he became closely identified with the Yakuza series.

The closure does not establish that Nagoshi himself is leaving the games industry. It does, however, end the first project announced by his independent studio. The reports do not provide a confirmed account of the project’s completion percentage, the total amount already spent, the future of its intellectual property, or what will happen to every member of the development team. Those details remain unknown unless the company, its former investor or another rights holder provides further information.

For now, the confirmed outcome is narrower but decisive: Nagoshi Studio was dissolved on October 31, NetEase had withdrawn its funding, and Gang of Dragon did not find a replacement backer. The game’s future beyond the closed studio has not been announced.
