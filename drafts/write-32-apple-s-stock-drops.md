# 苹果新CEO首次财报营收超预期，毛利率指引低于市场预期拖累股价

Apple 在新任首席执行官 John Ternus 上任后的首份季度财报中，公布了高于华尔街预期的营收和每股收益，但对下一季度毛利率的预测低于分析师预期。公司称，存储器等关键零部件成本上升将继续影响利润率。财报发布后，Apple 股价在盘后交易中下跌约3%，投资者开始评估新管理层面对供应约束和成本压力的应对能力。

## 营收与每股收益超过预期

CNBC 报道，Apple 2026财年第四季度营收为1,144.8亿美元，高于 LSEG 分析师预期的1,133.9亿美元；每股收益为2.10美元，也高于预期的1.99美元。公司营收同比增长12%。iPhone 收入为575.8亿美元，高于分析师预期；Mac 收入也超过预期。iPad 收入和可穿戴设备、家庭及配件收入则低于市场预测。

## 毛利率展望成为焦点

公司本季度毛利率为47.9%，略高于分析师预期的47.5%，但这一数字包含关税退款带来的约1个百分点正面影响。管理层预计，12月季度毛利率将在45%至46%之间，低于 LSEG 分析师平均预期的46.3%。CNBC 报道称，投资者担心更高的存储器成本以及人工智能功能相关支出会侵蚀利润率。

## 供应约束与零部件成本

CEO John Ternus 在电话会上表示，存储器价格上涨幅度前所未有。Apple 预计下一季度存储器成本还会进一步提高，尽管此前产品涨价、库存和部分非存储器组件成本下降可以提供一定缓冲。Ternus 还称，公司为改善供货而增加了制造采购承诺。供应问题不仅影响利润率，也限制了公司在需求旺盛时期向市场提供产品的能力。

## iPhone和服务业务表现分化

iPhone 收入达到575.8亿美元，超过分析师预期；Mac 收入为107.9亿美元，也高于市场预测。相较之下，iPad 收入为62.3亿美元，低于预期的67亿美元；可穿戴设备、家庭及配件收入为83亿美元，也低于预期的90.6亿美元。服务业务收入表现强劲，但管理层给出的下一季度服务收入增长预测为8%至9%，市场仍关注其增长速度。

## 新CEO面临的第一道考题

这是 Ternus 自9月1日接任 CEO 后的首份财报。Apple 的营收和每股收益显示其核心业务仍有韧性，但盘后股价下跌反映投资者更在意未来成本和利润率走势。公司预计12月季度营收同比增长7%至10%，但这一展望建立在全球关税政策和宏观环境不进一步恶化的假设之上。下一季度的实际供货、存储器成本和产品组合，将成为检验这一预测的关键。

# Apple Beats Revenue Estimates in John Ternus’ First Earnings Report, but Margin Outlook Weighs on Shares

Apple reported quarterly revenue and earnings above Wall Street expectations in the first earnings report under CEO John Ternus, but its forecast for the next quarter’s gross margin came in below analyst estimates. The company said higher memory costs and other component pressures would weigh on profitability. Apple shares fell about 3% in extended trading after the results, as investors focused on the balance between strong demand, constrained supply and rising costs.

## Revenue and earnings top estimates

CNBC reported that Apple generated $114.48 billion in revenue for its fiscal fourth quarter, compared with the $113.39 billion expected by analysts surveyed by LSEG. Earnings per share were $2.10, above the $1.99 estimate. Revenue increased 12% from a year earlier.

The results were mixed across product categories. iPhone revenue reached $57.58 billion, exceeding the $56.54 billion estimate. Mac revenue was $10.79 billion, above the $9.61 billion estimate. By contrast, iPad revenue of $6.23 billion fell short of the $6.7 billion estimate. Wearables, Home and Accessories generated $8.3 billion, below the $9.06 billion expected by analysts.

## Margin guidance becomes the main concern

Apple’s reported gross margin was 47.9%, slightly above the 47.5% analyst estimate cited by CNBC. However, the company said tariff refunds provided an approximately one-percentage-point benefit to the September-quarter margin. The December-quarter gross margin forecast of 45% to 46% was below the 46.3% average analyst estimate.

That guidance became a key reason for the negative share-price reaction. Investors are watching whether Apple can maintain profitability as the cost of memory and other components rises. The difference between the reported margin and the next-quarter outlook also highlights why a revenue beat does not necessarily translate into a positive market response.

## Supply constraints and component costs

Ternus told analysts that memory pricing was rising at an unprecedented pace. Apple expects memory costs to increase further in the December quarter. Price increases on some products, inventory carried into the quarter and lower costs for certain non-memory components are expected to offset part of the pressure, but not all of it.

The company is also working to improve product availability. Ternus said manufacturing purchase obligations increased by $33 billion from the June quarter to the September quarter. The commitment reflects efforts to secure supply, but it also underscores the scale of the company’s supply-chain planning as demand and component prices change.

## Product lines show different trends

The iPhone remained a major source of strength, with quarterly revenue above analyst expectations. Mac also outperformed forecasts. The weaker-than-expected iPad and Wearables, Home and Accessories figures show that demand was not equally strong across the portfolio.

Services remained an important part of the business, but Apple’s December-quarter forecast calls for services revenue growth of 8% to 9%. Investors will be watching whether that pace can be sustained while the company invests in new services and artificial-intelligence features. The earnings report does not establish that the services business is in decline; it provides management’s outlook for the coming quarter.

## A first test for the new CEO

Ternus became Apple’s chief executive on September 1, succeeding Tim Cook. The first report under his leadership showed continued revenue growth and earnings above expectations, but the market reaction reflected concerns about the cost environment and forward margins.

Apple expects December-quarter revenue to grow 7% to 10% year over year. The company said that outlook assumes current global tariff policies remain in effect and that the broader economic environment does not deteriorate. Actual performance will depend on product supply, memory prices and the mix of devices and services sold during the holiday period.

The immediate takeaway is not that Apple’s business has suddenly weakened. Rather, the results show that investors are looking beyond headline revenue growth to the cost of delivering that growth. The next quarter will test whether Apple can convert strong demand into sales without allowing higher component expenses to erode profitability.
