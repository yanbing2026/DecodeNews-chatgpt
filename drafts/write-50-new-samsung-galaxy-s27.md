# 认证资料显示Samsung Galaxy S27+仍支持45W有线充电，速度与前代相同

Samsung 尚未正式发布 Galaxy S27 系列，但 Galaxy S27+ 的新安全认证资料显示，该机支持最高45W有线充电。GSMArena 11月2日报道，相关认证列出15V、3A的额定参数，换算为45W，与 Galaxy S26+ 相同。这意味着 Samsung Plus 系列可能连续第六代维持这一充电功率。

## 新认证再次指向45W

GSMArena 报道称，Galaxy S27+ 此前已在中国3C数据库中出现45W充电信息，最新的安全认证进一步支持这一规格。认证资料列出15V和3A，按电压乘以电流计算为45W。不同地区型号也出现在认证清单中，包括全球版、韩国版、美国版、中国版以及面向日本市场的型号。

## Plus系列充电功率多年未变

GSMArena 指出，45W与 Galaxy S26+ 的有线充电功率相同。Samsung Plus 型号自 Galaxy S22+ 以来一直采用45W，若 S27+ 最终规格不变，将是连续第六代维持相同功率。基础款 Galaxy S27 的认证则指向25W，与前代基础款相同。

## 型号覆盖多个市场

报道提到，认证涵盖 SM-S956B、SM-S956N、SM-S956U、SM-S9560 等型号，并包括日本市场相关型号。不同地区型号通常对应不同销售渠道或运营商版本。认证资料显示多个版本采用相同充电参数，但这并不代表手机所有硬件和网络配置完全一致。

## 正式规格仍待Samsung确认

GSMArena 指出，Galaxy S27 系列预计在2027年1月或2月的常规发布窗口亮相，但这属于基于行业节奏的预期，并非 Samsung 已公布的发布日程。认证资料可以帮助确认部分硬件规格，但最终上市产品仍可能存在地区差异或后续调整。

## 充电速度不是整机体验的全部

45W有线充电意味着 S27+ 的充电功率可能与前代相同，但实际充电时间还会受到电池容量、充电曲线、温度管理和兼容充电器影响。当前认证并未提供完整电池容量或实际充电测试数据。因此，不能仅凭额定功率判断整机续航或实际充电表现。

# Samsung Galaxy S27+ Certification Points to 45W Charging, Matching Its Predecessor

Samsung has not officially announced the Galaxy S27 lineup, but new certification information indicates that the Galaxy S27+ may retain 45W wired charging. GSMArena reported on November 2 that safety certification documents list a 15-volt, 3-amp rating, which corresponds to 45 watts. That is the same rated charging power as the Galaxy S26+ and would continue a pattern across Samsung’s Plus models.

## New certification supports the 45W figure

The Galaxy S27+ had previously appeared in China’s 3C certification database with support for 45W charging. GSMArena said the latest safety certifications provide additional evidence for the same specification. The listed rating is 15V at 3A, which equals 45W.

The documents cover multiple regional versions of the handset, including global, Korean, U.S., Chinese and Japanese models. The certification information suggests that the rated charging speed will be consistent across those versions, although regional models can still differ in other hardware or network details.

## Plus models have kept the same charging power

GSMArena noted that 45W is the same wired charging rating offered by the Galaxy S26+. Samsung’s Plus models have used that power level since the Galaxy S22+. If the S27+ launches with the certified rating, it would be the sixth generation in a row to retain 45W charging.

The base Galaxy S27 is also expected to remain at 25W, based on its own certification information. That would match the charging rating of the Galaxy S26. The certification therefore points to continuity rather than a major charging upgrade for the standard and Plus models.

## Model numbers cover several markets

The report listed model numbers including SM-S956B, SM-S956N, SM-S956U and SM-S9560, alongside Japanese-market variants. These identifiers are used for different regional or distribution versions of the device. The presence of multiple model numbers in certification documents does not mean that every version will have identical network bands or other specifications.

The available information is focused on charging ratings. It does not provide a complete technical specification for the Galaxy S27+ or confirm every feature that will appear in the final retail device.

## Samsung has not confirmed a launch date

GSMArena said the Galaxy S27 series is expected to return to the usual January or February launch window. That is an expectation based on the company’s typical product cycle, not an official release date announced by Samsung. Certification documents can reveal technical details before a launch, but final retail specifications can still change.

The 45W rating should therefore be treated as a certification-based indication rather than a complete official product announcement. Samsung has not yet confirmed the phone’s full specifications, battery capacity or release schedule.

## Charging wattage does not determine the whole experience

A 45W rating describes the maximum rated wired charging power under specified conditions. Actual charging time also depends on battery capacity, charging curves, thermal management and the charger being used. The certification information does not provide real-world charging tests or a confirmed battery capacity.

The current evidence suggests that the Galaxy S27+ will not receive a higher wired charging rating than its predecessor. Whether Samsung makes other improvements to battery life or charging efficiency remains unknown until the company releases official specifications.
