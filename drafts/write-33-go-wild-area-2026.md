# Pokémon GO Wild Area 2026 将加入训练家背景展示和私人极巨化团体战

Niantic 为 Pokémon GO Wild Area 2026 公布一批新功能，包括在训练家个人资料中展示伙伴宝可梦的地点背景或特殊背景，以及让玩家通过私人团体代码加入 Dynamax Battles。官方还公布了活动特别研究、头像服饰、Pokémon HOME 转移支持和部分功能开放时间。相关更新将随11月的活动分阶段上线。

## 伙伴宝可梦背景将显示在个人资料

Pokémon GO 官方11月3日公告称，从 Wild Area 2026 开始，训练家可以在个人资料中展示伙伴宝可梦拥有的 Location Background 或 Special Background，好友也能看到这些背景。仙台和墨西哥城活动地点背景将于11月6日上午10时（当地时间）开放展示；Wild Area 2026 特殊背景及此前发布的地点背景则从11月9日上午10时开始支持。好友能否看到背景，还取决于其所在时区是否到达对应开放时间。

## Dynamax Battles 增加私人组队选项

官方宣布，玩家进入 Dynamax Battle 大厅后，将可以在自动匹配和 Private Group 之间选择。选择 Battle 会自动匹配其他玩家；选择 Private Group 则需要输入团体代码，加入已经建立的队伍。这一变化让朋友或固定团队能够自行组织战斗，不必完全依赖大厅自动匹配。该功能从本届 Wild Area 开始推出，并计划用于之后的活动。

## 特别研究开放钢系和龙系路线

在 Pokémon GO Wild Area: Global 活动期间，持票训练家可以参与分支式 Special Research，选择研究 Steel-type 或 Dragon-type 宝可梦。完成对应路线并获得活动奖章后，玩家捕捉相应类型的 mighty Pokémon 时可获得额外奖励。达到31级及以上的训练家可在捕捉钢系或龙系 mighty Pokémon 时额外获得1个 Candy XL；两条路线还分别提供额外1,000 Stardust。相关奖励在领取对应奖章后生效，并在全球活动结束时失效。

## 新头像物品和GO Pass安排

所有训练家都可以通过免费 GO Pass: Into the Wild! 解锁 GO Wild Area 2026 Tee（Purple）。升级至付费 GO Pass Deluxe: Into the Wild 的玩家，则可在升级进度中获得 Duraludon Pants。GO Pass 将于11月9日上午10时开放，至11月15日下午6时结束，均按当地时间计算。官方还列出可在游戏商店购买的 Duraludon Helmet、Gigantamax Duraludon Backpack 和 Fighting-Type Jacket。

## Pokémon HOME与冒险效果更新

官方同时宣布，玩家现在可以把使用 GO Safari Ball 捕获的宝可梦转移至 Pokémon HOME；在 Pokémon HOME 中，该球会显示为 Strange Ball。另一个更新允许玩家从 Into the Wild 活动开始，在 Raid 或 Max Battle 大厅内启动部分 Adventure Effects。训练家可以在战斗开始前使用 Zacian 的 Behemoth Blade 或 Zamazenta 的 Behemoth Bash 效果，为团队提供战斗增益。

这些功能围绕 Wild Area 2026 的全球活动和线下活动推出，部分奖励需要购票或参加指定地点活动。官方提醒玩家注意周边环境，活动安排也可能调整。具体可用时间以当地时间和游戏内实际开放状态为准。

# Pokémon GO Wild Area 2026 Adds Trainer Profile Backgrounds and Private Dynamax Battle Groups

Niantic has announced a new set of features for Pokémon GO Wild Area 2026, including the ability to display a Buddy Pokémon’s Location Background or Special Background on a Trainer profile and an option to join Dynamax Battles through private groups. The official announcement also details event research, avatar items, Pokémon HOME transfers and a phased schedule for the updates. Some rewards require an event ticket or participation in the in-person events.

## Buddy Pokémon backgrounds come to Trainer profiles

In an announcement published November 3, the Pokémon GO team said Trainers will be able to show an eligible Buddy Pokémon background on their profile, where friends can see it. The rollout will happen in stages. The GO Wild Area 2026 Location Backgrounds for Sendai and Mexico City become available to display on November 6 at 10 a.m. local time. The Special Background for the 2026 event and previously released Location Backgrounds follow on November 9 at 10 a.m. local time.

The feature is tied to local time. A friend will not be able to see a background until the scheduled release time has arrived in that friend’s own time zone. The announcement says additional Special Backgrounds will be supported in the future, although it does not provide a full schedule for those later additions.

## Dynamax Battles gain a private-group option

The update changes how players can enter Dynamax Battle lobbies. Trainers will be able to select the standard Battle option to be automatically matched with other players, or choose Private Group to enter an existing group by providing its code.

The new option is intended to make coordinated play easier for friends and groups that want to battle together. Previously, the lobby automatically matched players with other participants. The private option gives players a way to join a specific group rather than relying entirely on automatic matching. Niantic says the feature will begin with Wild Area 2026 and will also be available for future events.

## Special Research offers Steel and Dragon paths

During Pokémon GO Wild Area: Global, ticket-holding Trainers can take part in a branching Special Research story focused on either Steel-type or Dragon-type Pokémon. The selected path determines certain rewards and bonuses.

After earning the relevant event medal, Trainers who are level 31 or higher can receive one additional Candy XL when catching Mighty Pokémon of the selected type. The Steel Scholar and Dragon Defender paths also provide an additional 1,000 Stardust for catching the corresponding Mighty Pokémon. These bonuses begin after the player claims the relevant in-game medal and expire when the global event ends on November 15 at 6 p.m. local time.

Trainers attending the in-person events in Sendai and Mexico City who hold tickets will have an opportunity to collect both type-themed badges through their event Special Research. Their bonuses will remain active until the end of the global event, according to the announcement.

## Avatar items and GO Pass schedule

All Trainers can unlock the GO Wild Area 2026 Tee (Purple) through the free GO Pass: Into the Wild! Players who upgrade to GO Pass Deluxe: Into the Wild can earn Duraludon Pants as they progress. The pass becomes available on November 9 at 10 a.m. local time and runs through November 15 at 6 p.m. local time. Players can claim the listed avatar items and other rewards until November 17 at 11:59 p.m. local time.

The in-game shop will also offer a Duraludon Helmet, a Gigantamax Duraludon Backpack and a Fighting-Type Jacket. Ticket-holding Trainers who complete the event Special Research at the real-world events or the global event will receive a new avatar pose.

## Pokémon HOME transfers and Adventure Effects

The Pokémon GO team also said players can now transfer Pokémon caught in GO Safari Balls to Pokémon HOME. The ball will appear as a Strange Ball in Pokémon HOME.

Another change allows selected Adventure Effects to be activated while players are in a Raid or Max Battle lobby, beginning with the Into the Wild event. The announcement specifically mentions Zacian’s Behemoth Blade and Zamazenta’s Behemoth Bash as effects that players can activate before a battle begins. Ticket-holding participants at the Sendai and Mexico City events will receive early access to this feature.

The updates are tied to a mix of global and in-person event activities. Some bonuses and rewards require tickets, and the company says event details are subject to change. Players should check the game for the local availability of each feature and follow the event schedule in their region.
