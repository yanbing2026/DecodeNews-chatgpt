# 美股三大指数收盘涨跌不一，科技股抛售加深、油价大幅回落

美国股市11月2日收盘涨跌不一。投资者继续减持科技股，担心人工智能相关资本开支的回报，同时关注美国中期选举和本周企业财报。道琼斯工业平均指数上涨0.41%，标普500指数下跌0.51%，纳斯达克综合指数下跌1.07%。国际油价则因市场对美伊可能恢复外交接触的预期而下跌逾7%。

## 科技股拖累纳指和标普

CNBC 报道，纳斯达克综合指数收于25,164.32点，下跌1.07%；标普500指数收于7,541.40点，下跌0.51%。两项指数盘中一度上涨，随后随着科技股卖压扩大而回落。道指则上涨213.67点，涨幅0.41%，收于51,983.98点。资金从大型科技股转向部分传统行业，推动道指表现好于另外两项指数。

## 芯片和AI投资忧虑持续

市场继续评估科技公司在人工智能基础设施上的大额支出是否能够带来相应回报。CNBC 报道，Nvidia 和 Meta Platforms 股价均下跌约1%；Marvell Technology 下跌近13%，Lumentum 下跌约18%。两家公司宣布的交易引发投资者重新评估相关股票和行业估值。Oracle 股价也下跌约8%，此前公司公布了为人工智能建设融资的股票和债券发行计划。

## 油价因外交预期下跌

布伦特原油期货下跌7.63%，结算价为每桶104.77美元；美国西德克萨斯中质原油（WTI）下跌7.36%，结算价为每桶96.46美元。Reuters 报道，油价回落与市场对美国和伊朗可能恢复谈判的希望有关。若外交进展降低供应中断风险，市场对能源供应紧张的担忧可能缓和。不过，谈判前景仍存在不确定性，油价波动也可能迅速反转。

## 资金轮动支撑道指

道指上涨主要受到部分传统行业股票推动。CNBC 报道，Sherwin-Williams 和 Home Depot 分别上涨近6%和3.6%，Honeywell International、3M 和 Boeing 也上涨超过2%。与之相比，科技板块继续承压。这样的分化反映投资者在高估值科技股和相对传统行业之间调整仓位，而不是整个市场同步走强。

## 中期选举与财报增加波动

本周美国中期选举、就业数据和企业财报都可能成为市场波动因素。投资者不仅评估企业业绩，也关注政治结果可能带来的政策变化。Apple 在当天收盘后公布季度业绩，其股价此前已下跌近2%。市场对人工智能投资规模、通胀、利率和地缘政治的担忧仍然交织，短期走势可能继续受到消息驱动。

当天市场表现并非单一方向：道指上涨，标普和纳指下跌，油价大幅回落。科技股抛售显示市场对AI投资回报的疑虑仍在，而油价下跌则暂时缓解部分通胀压力。两种力量同时存在，使投资者在风险资产之间保持谨慎。

# Wall Street Ends Mixed as Tech Selling Deepens and Oil Prices Fall Sharply

U.S. stocks finished mixed on November 2 as investors continued to reduce exposure to technology shares and weighed concerns about the returns on artificial-intelligence investment. The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite fell. Oil prices dropped more than 7% amid hopes that the United States and Iran could resume diplomatic discussions. The session reflected competing forces: pressure on high-profile technology stocks and relief in energy markets.

## Technology shares weigh on the Nasdaq and S&P 500

The Nasdaq Composite fell 1.07% to close at 25,164.32, while the S&P 500 declined 0.51% to 7,541.40, according to CNBC’s November 2 market coverage. Both indexes had traded higher earlier in the session before selling in technology shares erased the gains. The Dow Jones Industrial Average advanced 213.67 points, or 0.41%, to finish at 51,983.98.

The divergence showed that the market was not moving as a single block. Investors continued to rotate away from some large technology companies and toward other sectors. The Dow’s relative strength reflected gains in several traditional businesses, even as technology-heavy indexes ended lower.

## Chip stocks and AI spending remain under scrutiny

Investors are questioning whether the large sums being committed to artificial-intelligence infrastructure will generate returns quickly enough to justify the spending. CNBC reported that shares of Nvidia and Meta Platforms each fell about 1%. Marvell Technology dropped nearly 13%, while Lumentum declined about 18%. The companies had announced a transaction that prompted investors to reassess the outlook for the businesses involved.

Oracle shares fell about 8% after the company outlined plans to sell $10 billion in common stock and $10 billion in bonds to finance its AI expansion. The moves reinforced concerns that technology companies may need to raise substantial amounts of capital to fund data-center and computing investments. The share-price declines do not establish that AI demand is weakening; they reflect investor debate over costs, financing and future returns.

## Oil falls on hopes for diplomatic progress

Brent crude futures fell 7.63% to settle at $104.77 a barrel. U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude dropped 7.36% to $96.46 a barrel, according to CNBC. Reuters’ market coverage attributed the decline to hopes that Washington and Tehran could resume diplomatic efforts.

A potential diplomatic path could reduce concerns about disruptions to energy supplies. However, the possibility of negotiations is not the same as a completed agreement, and the geopolitical situation remains uncertain. Oil prices can reverse quickly if talks stall or if the risk of supply disruption rises again.

## Sector rotation supports the Dow

The Dow’s gain was helped by strength in companies outside the technology sector. CNBC reported that Sherwin-Williams rose nearly 6% and Home Depot gained 3.6%. Honeywell International, 3M and Boeing also advanced by more than 2%.

The contrast with technology shares suggests investors were adjusting their exposure rather than buying the entire market indiscriminately. Some investors moved toward established industrial and consumer businesses while reassessing high-growth companies whose valuations are closely tied to expectations for AI investment.

## Elections and earnings add to market uncertainty

Investors are also preparing for the U.S. midterm elections, a key employment report and a busy earnings calendar. Political developments can affect expectations for fiscal policy and regulation, while corporate results provide information about demand and spending. Apple reported its quarterly results after the market close on November 2; its shares had already fallen nearly 2% during the regular session.

The market continues to face several overlapping risks, including concerns about AI capital spending, inflation, interest rates and geopolitics. The November 2 session captured that uncertainty: the Dow advanced, the S&P 500 and Nasdaq declined, and oil prices fell sharply.

The day’s moves do not point to a single market direction. Technology selling indicates that investors remain selective about AI-related valuations, while the oil decline temporarily eased one source of inflation pressure. Whether those trends continue will depend on company guidance, economic data and developments in U.S.-Iran relations.
