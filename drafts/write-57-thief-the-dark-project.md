# 《Thief: The Dark Project Remastered》确定12月3日发售，加入新战役和现代化功能

发行商 Atari 与开发团队 Nightdive Studios、Eidos Montreal 宣布，《Thief: The Dark Project Remastered》将于12月3日登陆 PlayStation 5、Xbox Series、Switch 2、PlayStation 4、Switch 和 PC。该作是1998年潜行游戏《Thief: The Dark Project》的现代重制版，采用 Nightdive 的 KEX Engine，并整合1999年《Thief: Gold》的额外任务和内容。

## 多平台发售日期确定

Gematsu 11月2日报道，游戏将于12月3日全球发售，平台包括PS5、Xbox Series、Switch 2、PS4、Switch，以及通过 Steam、Epic Games Store 和 GOG 发售的PC版本。发行商 Atari 与开发商 Nightdive Studios、Eidos Montreal 已开放预购。实体版也将通过零售渠道提供。

## 重制版保留经典潜行设计

原版《Thief: The Dark Project》于1998年推出，以光线和声音作为潜行机制。玩家扮演 Garrett，需要利用阴影和环境声音避开敌人，而不是像许多第一人称游戏那样直接战斗。Nightdive 表示，重制版保留原作关卡结构和核心潜行体验，同时提升画面、模型、动画和界面。

## 整合Thief Gold内容并加入新战役

Gematsu 援引开发团队介绍称，重制版包含原版12个大型任务，以及《Thief: Gold》增加的3个任务和5种新敌人。PC Gamer 另报道称，游戏还将加入名为《The Tarnished Mirror》的全新4任务战役，由曾制作《The Black Parade》大型模组的团队参与开发。新战役将扩展原作故事，并连接到《Thief 2: The Metal Age》的剧情。

## 画面和操作获得现代化改进

游戏使用 KEX Engine 更新纹理、模型和动画，支持最高4K分辨率和120帧画面。控制方面增加现代手柄支持、武器与物品轮盘、任务选择器和陀螺仪控制。PC版本还将支持自定义战役，让玩家能够使用社区制作的额外任务。

## 预购版本和价格

Gematsu 报道称，标准版价格为29.99美元，数字豪华版为34.99美元。豪华版包含游戏本体、艺术相关内容、原声音乐和小说预览等附加内容。不同地区价格以当地商店页面为准。发售日已确定，但具体平台性能表现和各版本差异仍需等待正式上市后的评测。

# Thief: The Dark Project Remastered Gets a December 3 Release Date With New Campaign Content

Atari and developers Nightdive Studios and Eidos Montreal have announced that Thief: The Dark Project Remastered will launch on December 3. The remaster is planned for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch and PC through Steam, the Epic Games Store and GOG. It revives the 1998 stealth game using Nightdive’s KEX Engine and includes content from the 1999 Thief: Gold release.

## Release date and platforms confirmed

Gematsu reported on November 2 that the game will launch on December 3 across the listed consoles and PC storefronts. Publisher Atari and developers Nightdive Studios and Eidos Montreal have opened preorders. Physical editions will also be available through retail channels.

The release brings the original stealth title to current and previous-generation platforms. The game’s return is aimed at players familiar with the original as well as audiences who may not have played the 1998 release.

## The original stealth design remains central

Thief: The Dark Project helped establish a style of first-person stealth built around light and sound. Players control Garrett, a thief who relies on shadows, quiet movement and careful timing to avoid detection. Rather than emphasizing direct combat, the game asks players to understand how enemies hear and see movement.

Nightdive says the remaster retains the original mission design while updating graphics and interface elements. The KEX Engine is being used to improve textures, models and animations, while the underlying stealth mechanics remain recognizable.

## Thief Gold content and a new campaign are included

Gematsu reported that the remaster includes the original Thief: The Dark Project campaign, which contains 12 missions, along with the additional content from Thief: Gold. The Gold update added three missions and five types of enemies.

PC Gamer also reported that the release will include a new four-mission campaign called The Tarnished Mirror. The expansion is being developed by the team behind The Black Parade, a major fan-made campaign. The new story is intended to connect the events of Thief: The Dark Project with Thief 2: The Metal Age.

## Visual and control improvements

The remaster uses Nightdive’s KEX Engine and includes updated textures, models and animations. Gematsu reported support for resolutions up to 4K and frame rates up to 120 frames per second. Modern controller support adds a weapon and item wheel, rumble and motion or gyro controls.

A mission selector will allow players to replay completed missions. The PC version will also support custom campaigns, allowing players to access additional content created by the community.

## Editions and preorder pricing

Gematsu reported a standard edition price of $29.99 and a Digital Deluxe Edition price of $34.99. The deluxe version includes the game, additional artwork-related material, the soundtrack and a preview of a Thief novel. Prices may differ by region and storefront.

The December 3 date is confirmed by the publisher and developers. Performance on each platform and the quality of the new campaign will be clearer after the game is released and reviewed. For now, the announcement establishes the release schedule, platform list and main additions included in the remaster.
