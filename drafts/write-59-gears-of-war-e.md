# 《Gears of War: E-Day》5亿美元预算说法可能包含工作室其他项目成本

围绕《Gears of War: E-Day》开发成本的争论，焦点从单款游戏是否花费5亿美元，转向 The Coalition 多年来承担的更广泛开发和技术工作。Windows Central 记者 Jez Corden 表示，他可以确认 Microsoft 财务审计将超过5亿美元成本归入 The Coalition，但这笔金额覆盖《Gears of War 5》到《E-Day》之间约六至七年的工作，并不等同于《E-Day》单独开发预算。

## 5亿美元数字需要看成本归属

Windows Central 11月2日报道，外界此前流传《Gears of War: E-Day》开发预算超过5亿美元。Corden 表示，Microsoft 审计账目确实将超过5亿美元归入 The Coalition，但该数字涉及工作室多个年度和不同项目的成本分摊。GameSpot 转述称，不能简单把这笔费用全部视为《E-Day》本身的直接制作支出。

## 工作室承担多个Xbox项目工作

Windows Central 报道称，The Coalition 在开发《E-Day》期间还为其他 Xbox 游戏提供支持，包括 Avowed、Hellblade 2、Clockwork Revolution、State of Decay 3、Grounded、Grounded 2，以及即将推出的 StarCraft 项目。该工作室也参与了 Unreal Engine 技术工具和集成工作，为其他团队提供开发支持。

## 技术研发和取消项目也计入成本

Corden 表示，The Coalition 曾与 Epic Games 合作制作 Matrix Awakens 和 Alpha Point 等 Unreal Engine 5 技术展示项目。相关工具和技术后来被用于其他第一方及第三方游戏。Windows Central 还称，工作室曾为 Xbox 开发一个持续一年半以上的原型项目，后来被取消。上述工作可能被计入工作室整体成本，但不等于都属于《E-Day》的直接开发。

## 预算归属存在内部争议

Windows Central 报道称，Microsoft 内部对成本应如何分摊也存在不同意见。Corden 认为，单一的5亿美元数字容易让外界误以为全部资金都用于《E-Day》，忽略了工作室为整个 Xbox 开发生态提供的技术和项目支持。GameSpot 同样强调，预算数字的含义取决于是否包含工具、引擎支持和其他项目工作。

## 裁员和工作室未来仍未确定

Corden 表示，他担心这笔成本数字可能被用于解释未来的裁员或削减，但同时称自己没有听说 Xbox 已计划关闭 The Coalition。该表述属于记者根据内部信息作出的判断，不是 Microsoft 已宣布的裁员或关闭决定。公司没有公开一份完整成本拆分表，也没有披露《E-Day》独立预算。

因此，5亿美元更准确地说是被归入 The Coalition 的多年工作成本，而不是已经证实的单款游戏预算。项目最终成本、各工作流占比以及未来人员安排，仍需等待 Microsoft 或工作室进一步披露。

# Gears of War: E-Day’s Reported $500 Million Budget May Include Work Beyond the Game

The debate over the reported cost of Gears of War: E-Day is increasingly focused on what the widely cited $500 million figure actually covers. Windows Central journalist Jez Corden said he could confirm that Microsoft’s financial auditors had attributed more than $500 million to The Coalition across the period spanning Gears of War 5 and E-Day. But he argued that the figure reflects years of work across multiple projects and technology efforts, rather than the direct production budget of E-Day alone.

## The $500 million figure needs context

Windows Central reported on November 2 that the figure had been circulating as a reported cost for Gears of War: E-Day. Corden said the amount was real as a studio-level accounting figure, but it covered roughly six to seven years of work by The Coalition. GameSpot’s report emphasized that the total should not be interpreted as the cost of making E-Day alone.

The distinction is important because a studio’s total operating and development costs can include work that supports other projects. Without a detailed breakdown, the full amount cannot be assigned to one game.

## The studio contributed to other Xbox projects

Windows Central reported that The Coalition worked on several other projects while developing E-Day. The list included Avowed, Hellblade 2, Clockwork Revolution, State of Decay 3, Grounded and Grounded 2, as well as the upcoming StarCraft project. The studio also supported technology and engine work used by other developers in the Xbox portfolio.

Those contributions mean that the studio’s total costs may reflect a broader role within Microsoft’s game-development organization. The reported figure therefore includes more than the direct work visible in E-Day’s own production.

## Technology research and a canceled project also contributed

Corden said The Coalition worked with Epic Games on Unreal Engine 5 technology demonstrations, including Matrix Awakens and Alpha Point. Windows Central reported that tools and techniques developed through those efforts were later used across other first-party and third-party games.

The outlet also reported that The Coalition spent more than a year and a half on an Xbox prototype project that was eventually canceled. Such work can contribute to a studio’s overall costs even if it does not result in a released game. The available reporting does not quantify how much of the $500 million was assigned to each activity.

## Internal disagreement over cost allocation

Windows Central said there were disagreements inside Microsoft about how the costs should be attributed. Corden’s argument is that a single headline figure can create the impression that all of the money went directly into E-Day, when the studio also provided technical support and development work for other projects.

GameSpot similarly described the figure as more complicated than a straightforward production budget. Without a public accounting breakdown, it is not possible to determine exactly how much of the reported total should be assigned to E-Day itself.

## Layoffs and the studio’s future remain uncertain

Corden said he was concerned that the $500 million figure could be used to justify future cuts or layoffs, but he also said he had not heard that Xbox was planning to close The Coalition. That is a journalist’s assessment based on sources, not an official announcement of layoffs or a studio closure.

Microsoft has not published a full breakdown of the costs attributed to The Coalition, nor has it confirmed a standalone E-Day budget. The most defensible description is that more than $500 million was reportedly attributed to the studio across several years and multiple responsibilities. The direct cost of E-Day, the share assigned to shared technology work and the studio’s future staffing plans remain unclear.
