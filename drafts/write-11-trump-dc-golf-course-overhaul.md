# 特朗普政府提交东波托马克高尔夫球场改造方案，成本与工期仍不明

## 改造方案进入联邦审查
特朗普政府已将华盛顿特区East Potomac Golf Links高尔夫球场改造方案提交联邦审查。The Washington Post报道，计划希望把现有场地改造成锦标赛级球场，并在Hains Point半岛周围建设海堤。方案还涉及移除树木，但没有说明高尔夫球场改造本身的总成本、施工时间和未来球费。公开的海堤工程预算为3.045亿美元，成为目前方案中最明确的金额。

## 海堤工程是方案的重要部分
East Potomac位于华盛顿特区的公共公园区域，Hains Point伸入波托马克河与阿纳卡斯蒂亚河交汇处。政府方案提出围绕半岛建设海堤，作为整体改造的一部分。海堤工程与球场设计虽在同一计划中出现，但不能把海堤预算当作高尔夫球场改造的总成本。政府提交的材料仍缺少球场工程的完整预算和资金来源。

## 公共空间用途引发关注
East Potomac长期是当地居民使用的公共休闲空间，除高尔夫外，也承载散步、骑行、钓鱼和野餐等活动。此前报道显示，政府提出把球场改造成可承办大型赛事的锦标赛场地。改造可能改变场地布局和公众使用方式，因此项目不仅涉及体育设施升级，也涉及公共土地如何分配、哪些既有活动会受到影响。

## 方案仍受法律与规划程序约束
Associated Press此前报道，司法部向法院提交文件，确认国家公园管理局计划将设计方案提交国家首都规划委员会审查，并拟从11月15日起关闭场地以开展工程。The Washington Post最新报道则指出，完整施工时间和球场成本仍未明确。联邦规划审查、相关诉讼及对树木移除的限制，意味着政府提交方案并不等于所有工程已经获准或可以立即全面开工。

## 关键问题仍未得到回答
目前最明确的工程金额是海堤预算，而不是整个改造项目的价格。球场何时关闭、施工持续多久、未来收费如何制定，以及公共休闲用途将保留多少，都关系到居民和使用者的实际影响。后续应关注规划委员会审查、法院对相关诉讼的处理，以及政府是否公布更完整的成本和施工计划。

# Trump Administration Submits East Potomac Golf Course Overhaul Plan, but Costs and Timing Remain Unclear

## The proposal enters federal review
The Trump administration has submitted a plan to overhaul East Potomac Golf Links in Washington, D.C., for federal review. The Washington Post reported that the proposal calls for a new championship-level golf course and a seawall around the Hains Point peninsula. It also includes tree removal. But the plan leaves several basic questions unanswered, including the total cost of the golf-course renovation, the construction schedule and the fees golfers would pay. The clearest figure in the proposal is the $304.5 million seawall project.

## The seawall is a major part of the plan
East Potomac is part of a public park area in Washington, with Hains Point extending into the waters near the confluence of the Potomac and Anacostia rivers. The administration’s proposal includes a seawall around the peninsula as part of the broader redevelopment. The seawall budget should not be confused with the cost of rebuilding the golf course itself. The available reporting says the proposal does not provide a complete budget or funding details for the golf project.

## Public access and existing uses are at issue
East Potomac has long served as a public recreational area. In addition to golf, people use the site for walking, cycling, fishing and picnicking. Earlier reporting described the administration’s goal of turning the course into a championship facility capable of hosting major events. Such a redesign could change the site’s layout and the ways members of the public use it. The dispute therefore involves more than a sports facility: it raises questions about how public land is allocated and which existing activities will remain available.

## Planning and legal review still matter
The Associated Press previously reported that the Justice Department filed a notice confirming that the National Park Service planned to submit a design to the National Capital Planning Commission and intended to close the course beginning November 15 for the project. The Washington Post’s November 2 report said the full construction timeline and golf-course cost remained unclear. Federal planning review, ongoing litigation and restrictions involving tree removal mean that submission of the proposal does not itself establish that every part of the project has been approved or can immediately proceed.

## Important questions remain unanswered
The seawall estimate is the most specific project cost reported so far, but it is not a total price for the redevelopment. Residents and users still need answers about when the course will close, how long construction will last, what future fees will be and how much public recreational access will be preserved. The next developments to watch are the planning commission’s review, court action in the related litigation and the release of a fuller cost and construction plan.
