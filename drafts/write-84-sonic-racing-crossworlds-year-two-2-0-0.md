《Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds》开启第二年更新：2.0.0加入Duel Mode与15种新Gadget

SEGA宣布，游戏第二年内容将于11月3日开启，首日推出2.0.0版本更新。主要变化包括1对1 Duel Mode、Grand Master Plate装备板、第七个Super Slot、15种新Gadget，以及World Match、每日任务和Festival活动调整。SEGA还公布了第二年的免费角色和付费联动路线图。报道时完整官方补丁说明尚未发布，平台推送状态应以游戏内提示为准。

## Duel Mode和装备系统

Duel Mode让两名玩家直接竞速。新Duel Boost能量会随时间和道具使用累积，落后者可发动加速。玩家可查看对手的道具、Gadget和位置。模式包括离线Rival Duel、在线Free Match、限时Best-of-10赛事和好友自定义比赛。Best-of-10属于限时活动，不是常驻队列。

Grand Master Plate提供第七个Super Slot，专门放置单格Gadget，并进一步增强该装备效果。更新还增加15种新Gadget，并调整装备效果、赛车属性、车辆类型和CPU对手。在线比赛中的Ring重新出现等待时间也会缩短，玩家可收藏Gadget并使用更多筛选功能。

## World Match和Festival调整

World Match加入Free Match，比赛结果不会影响排名；击败新加入的Rivals可获得额外Donpa Tickets。Play Missions允许玩家完成每日目标，获取Tickets和Medals。

Festival将从3天延长至10天，排名方式改为Winning Streak Rankings，并加入AI Rival Teams。在线比赛还会提供更多类型的Crown奖励。其他更新包括新音乐专辑、合作贴纸、装备收藏与筛选功能及错误修复。

## 新角色与联动内容

第二年首批免费角色包括Bayonetta、McLaren Racing（Sonic）和Aigis。Bayonetta计划于11月3日加入，可通过Rival Duel解锁；McLaren Racing安排在11月稍后推出，Aigis计划于2027年初加入。

付费Godzilla Pack包括一条新赛道、三辆主题赛车、额外音乐和音效，属于Season Pass Two，不是免费2.0.0更新的一部分。SEGA的后续路线图还包括EVANGELION、Virtual Sanrio Puroland、SEGALAND、Final Fantasy VII Remake和Persona等合作内容。

## 官方完整补丁说明仍待发布

SEGA、Steam官方公告和PlayStation Blog确认第二年内容从11月3日开始。Push Square在当天报道时表示，官方完整补丁说明尚未发布。因此，本文依据SEGA路线图、平台公告和媒体预览撰写，并不代表所有平台均已完成推送。玩家应检查游戏版本和平台商店通知，确认功能何时开放。

Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds Starts Year Two With Version 2.0.0, Adding Duel Mode and 15 New Gadgets

SEGA says the second year of Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds content begins on November 3 with Version 2.0.0. The update adds one-on-one Duel Mode, the Grand Master Plate and a seventh Super Slot, 15 new gadgets, and changes to World Match, daily missions and Festival events. SEGA has also outlined a roadmap of free characters and paid collaborations. At the time of reporting, the full official patch notes had not yet been published, so platform rollout status should be checked in-game.

## Duel Mode and the gadget system

Duel Mode puts two racers head-to-head. Its Duel Boost gauge fills over time and through item use, allowing the trailing racer to trigger a speed burst. Players can see an opponent's items, gadgets and position. Formats include offline Rival Duel, online Free Match, limited-time Best-of-10 events and customizable races with friends. Best-of-10 is a limited-time event, not a permanent queue.

The Grand Master Plate adds a seventh Super Slot reserved for one-slot gadgets and further boosts the effect of an item placed there. The update also adds 15 gadgets and adjusts gadget effects, racing stats, machine types and CPU opponents. Online ring respawn times will be shortened, and players can bookmark gadgets and use additional filters.

## World Match and Festival changes

World Match gains a Free Match option that does not affect rankings. Defeating new Rivals can earn additional Donpa Tickets. Play Missions lets players complete daily objectives to receive Tickets and Medals.

Festivals will last 10 days instead of three, and rankings will switch to Winning Streak Rankings. AI Rival Teams will be added, and online play will award more types of Crowns. Other changes include a new music album, collaboration decals, gadget bookmarking and filters, and bug fixes.

## New characters and crossover content

The first free characters announced for Year Two are Bayonetta, McLaren Racing (Sonic) and Aigis. Bayonetta is scheduled for November 3 and can be unlocked through Rival Duel. McLaren Racing is planned for later in November, while Aigis is scheduled for early 2027.

The paid Godzilla Pack includes a new course, three themed machines, additional music and sounds. It is part of Season Pass Two and is separate from the free Version 2.0.0 update. SEGA's longer-term roadmap also lists collaborations involving EVANGELION, Virtual Sanrio Puroland, SEGALAND, Final Fantasy VII Remake and Persona.

## Full official patch notes are still pending

SEGA, the official Steam announcement and PlayStation Blog confirmed that Year Two begins on November 3. Push Square reported that the full official patch notes had not yet been published at the time of its story that day. This article is based on SEGA's roadmap, platform announcements and media previews; it does not assume that every platform has completed its rollout. Players should check their game version and platform-store notices for availability.
