# 报道称Apple计划推出约千美元家用机器人，最早可能在2027年亮相

## Apple扩大智能家居与机器人研发
Ars Technica援引The Information报道，Apple正在开发一款可在家中自主移动的桌面机器人，内部代号为J595。该设备可能支持FaceTime通话，并在感知到有人进入房间时控制灯光。报道说，Apple新任CEO John Ternus支持家用机器人项目，但产品尚未正式公布，最终功能、名称和发布时间都可能变化。

## 设备可能配备可转动屏幕
根据报道，原型机采用约9英寸显示屏，并安装在可转动的机械臂上，让屏幕和摄像头能够朝向正在说话的人。与固定式智能屏幕不同，这种设计可以在视频通话时调整朝向，也可能结合摄像头和传感器感知周围环境。报道举例称，设备可能在有人进入房间时自动开灯，但这些仍是开发阶段的目标功能。

## 价格与上市时间尚未确定
The Information的消息人士称，Apple希望以约1,000美元或更高的价格推出该设备，目标时间为2027年，但开发延误可能将上市时间推迟至2028年。产品仍未正式发布，价格和时间都不是Apple确认的承诺。设备能否达到预期的自主移动、安全感知和家庭自动化能力，也取决于后续软件开发与测试。

## 软件开发进度成为主要难题
报道指出，机器人硬件原型较早开始开发，但团队仍在讨论首代产品究竟应具备哪些功能。消息人士称，负责机器人本体的专业人员规模较小，软件团队则更大。团队需要在FaceTime、智能家居控制和更复杂的AI交互之间确定产品重点。若首代功能过于有限，消费者是否愿意支付较高价格将成为商业挑战。

## Apple还探索其他家务机器人
报道还提到，Apple另有面向家庭任务的机器人研究，例如折叠衣物等需要机械操作的工作。这类项目与带屏幕的移动桌面机器人不同，可能涉及机械臂和物体操控。现阶段这些项目均属于研发方向，并不代表Apple已决定推出通用家务机器人。未来应关注正式产品公告、实际功能演示和上市计划，而不是把内部研发目标当成已确定产品。

# Apple Reportedly Developing a Home Robot Priced Around $1,000, With a Possible 2027 Launch

## Apple expands its smart-home and robotics ambitions
Ars Technica, citing reporting by The Information, says Apple is developing a home robot that could move around a household autonomously. The device is internally code-named J595 and may support FaceTime calls and control room lighting when it detects someone entering. The report says new CEO John Ternus is backing the home-robot project. Apple has not announced the product, however, and its final features, name and release timing could change.

## The device may use a swiveling display
The reported prototype has a display of about 9 inches mounted on a movable arm. That design could let the screen and camera turn toward a person speaking during a video call. Unlike a stationary smart display, the device may also use cameras and sensors to understand its surroundings. The Information’s examples include switching on lights when someone enters a room. Those capabilities remain development targets, not confirmed retail features.

## Price and launch timing are not settled
People familiar with the project told The Information that Apple is aiming for a price of around $1,000 or more and a possible launch in 2027. Development delays could push the release to 2028. The company has not confirmed a price, launch window or product name. Whether the device can reliably navigate a home, recognize its surroundings and perform useful automation tasks will depend on further software development and testing.

## Software development is a major challenge
The report says Apple began developing early hardware prototypes years ago, but teams are still debating which features should be included in the first generation. Sources described a relatively small group of specialists focused on robotics and a larger software team. The company must decide how much to prioritize FaceTime, smart-home controls and more advanced AI interactions. If the first version offers only a limited set of functions, Apple will have to persuade consumers that the experience justifies a premium price.

## Apple is also exploring other household robots
The reporting also describes separate Apple research into robots that could perform household tasks such as folding clothes. That work differs from the display-equipped home robot and could require robotic arms and object manipulation. Both projects remain research and development efforts; they do not mean Apple has committed to releasing a general-purpose household robot. The next meaningful evidence will be an official product announcement, demonstrations of real-world capabilities and a confirmed release plan.
