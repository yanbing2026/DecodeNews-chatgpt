# 《Borderlands》系列销量突破1亿份，Gearbox称明年将继续推出《Borderlands 4》内容

Gearbox Software 宣布，《Borderlands》整个系列累计销量已超过1亿份，并表示团队正在为2027年准备更多《Borderlands 4》内容。这一里程碑覆盖系列全部作品，而不是单独一款游戏。公司没有公布各代作品的销量拆分，也没有说明下一阶段内容的具体名称和发布日期。

## 系列累计销量跨过1亿份

Gearbox 在面向玩家的社区消息中宣布，Borderlands franchise 已正式超过1亿份销量。Eurogamer 和 KitGuru 11月2日报道了这一消息。该系列最初作品于2009年推出，随后发展出多款正传、衍生作品和扩展内容。Gearbox 没有公开1亿份销量在不同作品之间的分布，因此不能把这一数字直接视为《Borderlands 4》的销量。

## 工作室确认2027年仍有新内容

Gearbox 在公告中表示，团队正在为明年准备更多《Borderlands 4》内容，并称“the team is cooking hard on more Borderlands 4 content for next year”。这一说法确认了后续支持方向，但没有给出具体内容清单、上线日期或是否会以大型资料片形式推出。玩家可以确认游戏还会继续获得内容更新，但目前无法判断更新的规模和节奏。

## 第一年的内容支持已告一段落

KitGuru 报道称，《Borderlands 4》首年支持包括两款 Story Packs、五款 Bounty Packs、两名新增 Vault Hunters、两场免费 Raid Boss 遭遇，以及终局 Takedown 活动和多项品质改进。Gearbox 还表示，周期性活动和社区挑战将继续进行。公司没有在这次销量公告中披露每项内容的具体销售收入或玩家参与数据。

## 系列未来方向仍在讨论

Gearbox 还表示，正在重新审视整个系列的未来，包括角色故事、值得继续讲述的剧情以及玩家希望看到哪些角色获得更多发展。公司邀请玩家提出意见，但没有宣布新的正传游戏、衍生项目或开发时间表。此类表述代表工作室正在征求社区反馈，不等于已经批准某一款新作品。

## 销量里程碑不等于单作商业表现

超过1亿份的累计销量说明系列长期拥有庞大的销售基础，但由于统计涵盖多个游戏和内容形态，不能据此推算《Borderlands 4》单独销量。Gearbox 也没有公布不同平台、地区或折扣销售的拆分。下一阶段值得关注的重点，是2027年内容的具体安排，以及系列在当前作品之后会采取怎样的发展方向。

# Borderlands Franchise Surpasses 100 Million Sales as Gearbox Plans More Borderlands 4 Content for 2027

Gearbox Software says the Borderlands franchise has surpassed 100 million units sold across the series, while the studio is preparing additional Borderlands 4 content for 2027. The milestone covers the franchise as a whole, not a single game. Gearbox has not provided a breakdown by title or platform, and it has not announced the names or release dates of the content planned for next year.

## Franchise sales pass the 100 million mark

Gearbox announced in a message to the Borderlands community that the franchise had officially crossed 100 million units sold. Eurogamer and KitGuru reported the announcement on November 2. The original Borderlands game was released in 2009, and the series has since expanded through numbered entries, spin-offs and additional content.

The company did not disclose how the total is divided among the games. The 100 million figure therefore should not be presented as the sales total for Borderlands 4. It is a cumulative franchise milestone that includes the broader series.

## More Borderlands 4 content is planned for 2027

Gearbox said the team is working on more Borderlands 4 content for next year, describing the development effort as active. The announcement signals continued support for the current game, but it does not identify specific projects, release dates or the format of the planned updates.

The statement does not clarify whether the 2027 content will consist of major story expansions, smaller additions or a combination of different releases. Players know that more content is planned, but the scale and timing remain open questions.

## The first year of post-launch support

KitGuru reported that Borderlands 4’s first year of support included two Story Packs, five Bounty Packs, two additional Vault Hunters, two free Raid Boss encounters and an endgame Takedown activity, alongside quality-of-life improvements and gameplay updates. Gearbox also said recurring activities and community challenges would continue.

The studio did not provide revenue figures or player-engagement data for those releases in the franchise milestone announcement. The list describes the types of content delivered, but it does not establish how much each item contributed to sales or how many players participated.

## Gearbox is also considering the franchise’s longer-term direction

The company said it is taking a broader look at the Borderlands universe, including which characters deserve more attention and which stories should receive further development. Gearbox invited the community to share ideas and feedback. However, the announcement did not reveal a new numbered game, a spin-off or a confirmed production schedule for another project.

The distinction between exploring ideas and announcing a project is important. The message indicates that the studio is considering the series’ future, but it does not mean that a particular sequel or character-focused release has been approved.

## A franchise milestone is not a single-game sales report

The 100 million-unit figure demonstrates the long-term commercial reach of the Borderlands brand. But because it aggregates multiple releases, it cannot be used to calculate Borderlands 4’s individual sales. Gearbox did not publish a title-by-title breakdown, regional split or platform-specific total.

The next concrete information to watch for will be the details of the 2027 Borderlands 4 content plan and any decisions about the wider franchise. For now, the company has confirmed a cumulative sales milestone and a commitment to continue supporting the current game, while leaving the next releases unspecified.
