# 《World of Warcraft: Forever》11月4日全球上线，北美东部时间晚6时开放

Blizzard 宣布，《World of Warcraft: Forever》将于11月4日全球上线。根据 Blizzard 官方时间表，美洲地区以太平洋时间下午3时开放；美国东部时间为当日下午6时。欧洲部分地区、亚洲和澳大利亚则因时区差异在11月5日进入游戏。该版本以2004年《World of Warcraft》初始体验为基础，同时加入新区域、任务、种族和系统调整。

## 全球开放时间按地区换算

Polygon 11月2日报道，WoW: Forever 将于11月4日美国东部时间下午6时全球开放。美国西海岸为太平洋时间下午3时，英国为格林威治标准时间晚上11时，欧洲中部时间则已进入11月5日凌晨。日本和韩国分别在11月5日上午8时和9时开放。Blizzard 官方公告也列出了台湾地区时间为11月5日上午7时。

## 以经典版本为基础重新设计

Blizzard 表示，Forever 以2004年游戏初始时期的设计理念为基础，玩家可以重新探索原始大陆并升至60级。与传统经典服务器不同，这一版本加入新故事、任务、区域和重新平衡的职业。Polygon 报道称，游戏还提供新的 Skyborne 种族、现代化界面、手柄支持和重新设计的天赋系统。

## 新区域与任务扩展世界

Blizzard 官方公告称，游戏将扩展 Eastern Kingdoms 和 Kalimdor，并在1至60级旅程中加入超过1,000个新任务。新地点包括 Mount Hyjal、Shen’dralas、Riverglades 和 Zephras Isle。不同区域提供新的探索路线、故事和任务内容，使玩家既能体验熟悉的 Azeroth，也能进入新区域。

## 新种族与等级上限

Forever 新增 Skyborne 种族，并允许玩家选择加入 Horde 或 Alliance。Blizzard 表示，Skyborne 有不同阵营和职业路线。Polygon 还指出，游戏设定60级硬上限，开发团队承诺不会提高等级上限，设计目标是让玩家关注升级旅程，而不是不断追逐更高等级的终局内容。

## 订阅资格与额外内容

Blizzard 官方公告称，WoW: Forever 的基础访问包含在 World of Warcraft 订阅或游戏时间内。部分升级礼包和特定内容则需要额外购买。官方也说明了不同地区开放时间，避免玩家因跨时区日期变化错过上线。对于美东玩家而言，最重要的时间是11月4日下午6时。

# World of Warcraft: Forever Launches Globally on November 4, With an 6 p.m. Eastern Start

Blizzard has confirmed that World of Warcraft: Forever will launch globally on November 4. The official schedule lists a 3 p.m. Pacific start for the Americas, which corresponds to 6 p.m. Eastern time. Players in parts of Europe, Asia and Australia will see the launch occur on November 5 because of time-zone differences. The new version is based on the experience of World of Warcraft at its 2004 launch, but adds new zones, quests, character options and system changes.

## Global launch times vary by region

Polygon reported on November 2 that World of Warcraft: Forever will become available worldwide at 6 p.m. Eastern time on November 4. The launch is scheduled for 3 p.m. Pacific time on the U.S. West Coast and 11 p.m. Greenwich Mean Time in the United Kingdom. Central Europe reaches midnight on November 5, while Japan and Korea enter the game on the morning of November 5.

Blizzard’s official schedule lists Taiwan’s launch time as 7 a.m. on November 5. The regional schedule is important because the global launch occurs at one moment, but local calendar dates differ across regions.

## A redesigned version of the original experience

Blizzard describes Forever as a permanent home alongside the modern game and Classic servers. It is built around the original continents and a level-60 journey, while adding new stories, experiences and updates. Polygon reported that the game includes new content, rebalanced classes, graphical enhancements and modernized features.

The design is intended to recall the feel of the original game while incorporating changes that were not part of the 2004 launch version. The result is not simply a return to the old servers; it is a revised experience that uses the original world as its foundation.

## New zones expand the world

Blizzard’s official announcement says the game expands the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor with more than 1,000 new quests across the level 1-to-60 journey. New destinations include Mount Hyjal, Shen’dralas, the Riverglades and Zephras Isle.

The new locations add additional stories and routes for players to explore. They are designed to fit into the broader world while providing new content for characters progressing through the original level range.

## A new race and a level cap

World of Warcraft: Forever introduces the Skyborne as a playable race. Blizzard says players can choose different faction and class paths for the new characters. Polygon also reported that the game has a hard level cap of 60, which Blizzard has said it does not plan to raise.

The fixed cap reflects a design goal of emphasizing the journey through the world rather than a continual race toward higher endgame levels. Other changes include a modernized interface, gamepad support and redesigned class talent systems.

## Subscription access and optional upgrades

Blizzard says access to the base version of Forever is included with a World of Warcraft subscription or game time. Optional upgrade packs are available separately. The official announcement provides the regional launch schedule so players can identify the correct local time.

For players on the U.S. East Coast, the key time is 6 p.m. on November 4. Players in other regions should use Blizzard’s local schedule because the same global launch falls on the following calendar day in several time zones.
