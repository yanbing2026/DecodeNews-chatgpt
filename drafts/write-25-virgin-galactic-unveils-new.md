# Virgin Galactic公布新一代飞船Apeiron，目标2027年恢复商业太空飞行

## 新飞船抵达Spaceport America
Virgin Galactic公布新一代Delta级飞船Apeiron，并将其运抵新墨西哥州的Spaceport America。公司表示，飞船接下来将进行航电软件整合、验证和飞行测试，为恢复商业太空飞行做准备。Fox Business报道，公司计划在测试顺利完成后，于2027年第二季度初开始搭载客户飞行。这将是公司暂停商业运营、集中开发新一代飞船后的重要节点。

## 商业运营时间仍取决于测试
Virgin Galactic称，Apeiron预计在2027年第二季度初投入商业飞行，第二艘新一代飞船预计在第二季度末至第三季度初开始商业运营。公司强调，时间表以完成飞行测试和其他开发工作为前提。此前商业飞行暂停期间，Virgin Galactic将资源集中于新一代飞船研发，因此能否按计划完成测试将直接影响恢复运营的时间。

## 设计目标是提高飞行频率
公司称，新飞船的设计目标是每周最多飞行两次，整个运营寿命预计可执行超过500次任务。相较上一代飞船，这一设计意在提高航班频率和运营规模。不过，这些数字属于公司的设计目标，并非已经实现的实际飞行记录。飞行测试、维护周期和运营安全要求，都会影响最终能达到的频率。

## 名称来自全球公开征集
Virgin Galactic表示，Apeiron这个名称来自古希腊语，意为“无边界”。公司通过公开投票从候选名称中选出该名称，活动收到来自80多个国家、六大洲的参与者反馈。飞船先在亚利桑那州Mesa的工厂亮相，随后由母机Eve携带飞往新墨西哥州的运营基地。该过程标志着飞船从制造阶段进入基地整备和测试阶段。

## 恢复商业飞行仍面临执行风险
Virgin Galactic上一次商业太空飞行是在2024年，此后暂停运营以开发新一代飞船。公司表示，Apeiron将先完成航电软件验证和飞行测试，再开始载客商业服务。测试结果、生产进度以及第二艘飞船的准备情况，都会影响收入恢复节奏。2027年的目标日期仍是计划而非保证，后续应以公司正式项目更新和实际测试进展为准。

# Virgin Galactic Unveils New Spaceship Apeiron, Targeting a Return to Commercial Spaceflight in 2027

## The new spacecraft arrives at Spaceport America
Virgin Galactic has unveiled Apeiron, the lead vehicle in its next-generation Delta-class fleet, and flown it to Spaceport America in New Mexico. The company says the spacecraft will undergo avionics software integration, validation and flight-test preparations at the site. Fox Business reported that Virgin Galactic is targeting the beginning of commercial passenger flights in early second-quarter 2027, provided the test program is completed successfully. The milestone comes after the company paused commercial operations to focus on developing its next-generation spacecraft.

## Commercial service depends on successful testing
Virgin Galactic expects Apeiron to begin commercial operations in early Q2 2027. A second next-generation spacecraft is expected to enter commercial service in late Q2 or early Q3 2027. The company has stressed that the schedule depends on completing flight tests and other development work. Virgin Galactic suspended commercial flights while it concentrated on the new fleet, so the pace and results of testing will determine when the company can resume carrying customers.

## The design aims to increase flight frequency
The company says the new spacecraft is designed to fly as often as twice per week and complete more than 500 missions over its operational life. Those targets are intended to support a higher flight cadence and a larger commercial operation than the previous generation. They are design goals, not an established operating record. Flight-test results, maintenance requirements and operational safety procedures will affect how frequently the vehicle can actually fly.

## The name was selected through a public vote
Virgin Galactic says Apeiron comes from an ancient Greek word meaning “boundless.” The name was selected through a public vote that drew responses from people in more than 80 countries across six continents. The spacecraft was first revealed at the company’s factory in Mesa, Arizona, before being carried by the mothership Eve to its operating base in New Mexico. The move takes the vehicle from manufacturing and presentation into site preparation and testing.

## A return to commercial flights still carries execution risks
Virgin Galactic’s last commercial spaceflight took place in 2024. The company then paused operations to focus on its next-generation spacecraft. Apeiron must complete avionics validation and flight testing before it can begin carrying paying customers. Test results, production progress and preparation of the second spacecraft will all affect the timing of a revenue recovery. The 2027 target remains a plan rather than a guarantee, and further updates from the company will be needed to assess whether it is on schedule.
