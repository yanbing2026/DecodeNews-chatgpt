# Apple新CEO Ternus首份财报收入超预期，服务业务与成本成为焦点

## 新任CEO公布首份季度业绩
Apple公布截至2026年9月26日的第四季度业绩，这是John Ternus接任CEO后的首份财报。公司季度收入为1,145亿美元，同比增长12%；稀释后每股收益为2.10美元，同比增长14%。Apple表示，本季度总收入、iPhone收入和每股收益均创下9月季度纪录，服务业务收入也达到历史新高。

## iPhone与Mac收入超过预期
CNBC报道，iPhone收入为575.8亿美元，高于分析师预期的565.4亿美元；Mac收入为107.9亿美元，也高于预期。iPad收入为62.3亿美元，低于预期的67亿美元。新款iPhone Duo在本季度结束后才开始销售，因此没有计入本次业绩。Apple表示，iPhone收入同比增长，反映其主力产品线仍保持较强表现。

## 服务业务与成本指引受关注
Apple预计12月季度服务收入增长8%至9%。公司同时预计下一季度毛利率为45%至46%，低于分析师平均预期的46.3%。Apple表示，内存和其他零部件成本上升。公司已调整部分产品价格以抵消成本压力，但成本变化仍可能影响未来季度的利润率表现。

## Ternus谈到AI和硬件升级
Ternus在财报电话会上表示，Apple有机会通过硬件升级推动AI功能普及，并称新功能可以成为消费者更换设备的理由。公司在本季度推出更新版Siri AI，并继续扩展产品线。投资者和消费者将关注AI功能是否能带来更多设备升级，以及公司能否把新的软件体验转化为服务业务增长。

## 现金流和股东回报保持强劲
Apple表示，本季度经营现金流为280亿美元，并向股东返还280亿美元。董事会还宣布每股0.27美元的现金股息，计划于11月16日支付给截至11月12日登记在册的股东。公司同时称，活跃设备安装基数在所有主要产品类别和地区都达到新高。这些数据说明，除了销售增长，现金创造和用户生态规模仍是Apple财务表现的重要部分。

## 全年业绩与后续产品周期
Apple表示，2026财年全年收入达到4,790亿美元，创公司纪录。Ternus称，公司刚刚结束一个产品发布密集的阶段，包括新款iPhone、家庭产品和首次加入触控功能的Mac电脑。下一阶段的重点将是新产品上市表现、服务业务增长，以及零部件成本变化对利润率的影响。

# Apple Revenue Beats Expectations in New CEO John Ternus’ First Earnings Report

## The new CEO reports his first quarter
Apple reported results for the quarter ended September 26, 2026, giving CEO John Ternus his first earnings report since taking the role. Quarterly revenue was $114.5 billion, up 12% year over year, while diluted earnings per share reached $2.10, an increase of 14%. Apple said the September quarter set records for total company revenue, iPhone revenue and earnings per share. Services revenue also reached a new all-time high.

## iPhone and Mac revenue exceed estimates
CNBC reported that iPhone revenue was $57.58 billion, above analyst estimates of $56.54 billion. Mac revenue was $10.79 billion, also ahead of expectations. iPad revenue came in at $6.23 billion, below the $6.7 billion estimate. The new iPhone Duo went on sale after the quarter ended and was not included in the results. Apple said iPhone revenue increased year over year, reflecting continued strength in its largest product category.

## Services and component costs draw attention
Apple forecast services revenue growth of 8% to 9% for the December quarter. It also projected a gross margin of 45% to 46%, below the analyst average of 46.3% cited by CNBC. Apple said memory and other component costs had increased. The company has adjusted some product prices to offset cost pressure, but changes in component expenses could still affect margins in coming quarters.

## Ternus discusses AI and hardware upgrades
During the earnings call, Ternus said Apple sees opportunities to expand artificial intelligence through hardware, including by giving customers a reason to upgrade or switch devices. Apple released an updated Siri AI during the quarter and continued to expand its product lineup. Customers and investors will be watching whether new AI features encourage device upgrades and whether the company can translate new software experiences into growth for its services business.

## Cash generation and shareholder returns remain strong
Apple said it generated $28 billion in operating cash flow during the quarter and returned $28 billion to shareholders. Its board declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share, payable on November 16 to shareholders of record on November 12. The company also said its installed base of active devices reached a new all-time high across major product categories and geographic segments. These figures show that cash generation and the scale of Apple’s device ecosystem remain important parts of its financial performance.

## Full-year results and the next product cycle
Apple said revenue for fiscal 2026 reached $479 billion, a company record. Ternus said the company had completed a busy period of product launches, including new iPhones, home products and the first Mac computers with touch capabilities. The next phase will depend on the reception of new products, continued growth in services and the effect of component costs on profitability.
