Rockstar确认《Grand Theft Auto VI》预载时间与容量：最高需要212GB空间

Rockstar Games于11月3日公布《Grand Theft Auto VI》（GTA VI）的数字版预载安排、所需存储空间，以及实体盒装版本和Leonida地图海报的发放方式。PlayStation 5玩家可从11月10日当地时间午夜开始预载，Xbox Series X|S则于同日格林尼治标准时间零点开放。Rockstar表示，预载文件根据设备不同最高需要212GB可用空间。游戏计划于11月19日登陆PS5和Xbox Series X|S。

## PS5与Xbox预载时区不同

Rockstar官方预载FAQ确认，PS5数字版预载将在11月10日各地区当地时间午夜开始；Xbox Series X|S则按全球统一时间，于11月10日00:00 GMT开始。对美国东部玩家而言，Xbox预载时间对应11月9日晚上7点，而PS5要等到当地11月10日零点。两种平台的预载时刻不同，玩家应按自己的主机平台和地区安排下载。

Rockstar表示，PS5用户需要开启自动下载，并确保主机处于开机或休息模式；Xbox用户需要开启自动更新，并让主机保持开机或睡眠模式。PlayStation Blog也建议玩家提前检查PlayStation账户、预购状态和主机存储空间，以免在开放预载后才开始处理设置问题。

## 预载文件最高需要212GB

Rockstar支持页面和公告写明，GTA VI预载文件需要的可用空间最高为212GB，实际需求取决于设备。这个数字是官方公布的预载空间要求，不应直接等同于所有平台最终安装后占用的精确容量。GamesRadar提醒，不同平台的下载文件和最终安装容量可能存在差别，后续更新也可能改变实际占用空间。

对于存储空间有限的主机用户，提前清理不常玩的游戏或检查扩展存储是否可用，可能有助于预留足够空间。不过，Rockstar目前公布的是“最高212GB”的要求，并未在该公告中给出PS5与Xbox各自最终文件大小的独立数字。

## 实体盒装版没有游戏光盘

Rockstar确认，两个主机平台的实体盒装版本预计从11月12日起由零售商提供，但盒内是游戏下载代码，不包含实体游戏光盘。玩家兑换代码后可按平台规则预载游戏。Rockstar此前的产品说明也将实体版本描述为code-in-box形式，因此购买实体盒装版并不意味着可以通过光盘安装或离线保存游戏。

PlayStation Blog的说明聚焦PS5数字版预载准备；Game Informer和Push Square也指出，实体盒装版提供的是下载代码。购买前应确认自己拥有足够存储空间，并留意代码的地区限制和兑换要求。

## Leonida地图与封面海报另行提供

Rockstar表示，将于11月19日在官方网站提供可免费下载和打印的GTA VI封面海报及Leonida地图。实体版则是一张双面海报，计划在游戏上市后不久通过Rockstar Store销售，单独购买价格为0.99美元；在Rockstar Store购买其他商品时，也可作为赠品获得。它不会随实体盒装游戏一同附送。

因此，玩家若希望获得纸质地图，需要在游戏盒装版、官方数字文件和Rockstar Store实体海报之间作出区分。Push Square特别指出，实体盒装版不附带地图；The Verge也强调，实体游戏版本实际上仍是盒内兑换码，而非光盘。

## 发售日期仍为11月19日

Rockstar目前公布的预载安排并未改变游戏发售日期。GTA VI仍计划于11月19日在PlayStation 5和Xbox Series X|S推出。预载功能让已预购玩家可以提前下载文件，但不能提前进入游戏。不同地区的主机商店、自动下载设置和代码兑换规则可能影响实际准备过程，玩家应以Rockstar支持页面和各平台官方说明为准。

Rockstar Confirms Grand Theft Auto VI Preload Times and Storage Requirement of Up to 212GB

Rockstar Games announced on November 3 the digital preloading schedule for Grand Theft Auto VI, the amount of storage players may need, and details about physical boxed editions and a separate Leonida map poster. PlayStation 5 players can begin preloading at midnight local time on November 10. Xbox Series X|S players can start at 12 a.m. GMT on the same date. Rockstar says the preload file will require up to 212GB of available storage, depending on the device. The game remains scheduled to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## PS5 and Xbox use different preload clocks

Rockstar's official preload FAQ confirms that digital preloading on PS5 will begin at midnight on November 10 in each player's local time zone. Xbox Series X|S will use a single global start time of 12 a.m. GMT on November 10. For players in the US Eastern time zone, the Xbox preload begins at 7 p.m. on November 9, while the PS5 preload begins at midnight at the start of November 10. Players should therefore check the schedule for their own platform rather than assuming both versions unlock at the same local time.

Rockstar says PS5 owners must enable automatic downloads and keep the console either powered on or in Rest Mode. Xbox owners must enable automatic updates and keep the console powered on or in Sleep mode. PlayStation Blog also advises players to check their PlayStation account, preorder status and available console storage before the preload window opens, so that account or system settings do not delay the download.

## The preload requires up to 212GB

Rockstar's announcement and support page state that the preload file will require up to 212GB of available space, depending on the device. That is the official storage requirement for preloading; it should not be treated as a precise, identical installed size for every platform. GamesRadar noted that download and final installation sizes can differ across platforms and that later updates may also affect the amount of storage used.

Players with limited console storage may want to remove games they no longer play or check whether compatible expansion storage is available. However, Rockstar has announced a maximum requirement of 212GB rather than separate final file-size figures for the PS5 and Xbox versions. Reports that present 212GB as a fixed size for every console go beyond the wording of the official announcement.

## Physical boxed editions contain a download code, not a disc

Rockstar says physical boxed versions for both consoles should become available from retailers beginning November 12. The box will contain a download code rather than a physical game disc. After redeeming the code, players can preload the game according to the relevant platform's instructions. Rockstar's earlier product information also describes the physical edition as a code-in-box release, meaning that buying a boxed copy does not provide a disc for installation or offline storage.

PlayStation Blog focuses on preparing the PS5 digital version for preload, while Game Informer and Push Square also report that the physical boxed edition contains a download code. Buyers should make sure they have enough storage and should check regional code restrictions and redemption requirements before purchasing.

## The Leonida map and cover poster are separate items

Rockstar says it will add a printable Grand Theft Auto VI cover-art poster and a map of Leonida to its official website as a free digital download on November 19. A double-sided physical version will be offered shortly after launch through the Rockstar Store. It will cost $0.99 when purchased separately or be available as a free gift with another purchase from the Rockstar Store. The physical poster will not be included in the boxed game edition.

Players should distinguish among the boxed game, the free printable files and the separately distributed physical poster. Push Square specifically noted that the physical boxed edition will not include the map. The Verge likewise emphasized that the physical game release is still a download code in a box rather than a disc-based edition.

## The November 19 launch date remains unchanged

The preload announcement does not change the game's release date. Grand Theft Auto VI remains scheduled to launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19. Preloading allows eligible players to download the game files in advance, but it does not provide early access to play. Regional store arrangements, automatic-download settings and code-redemption rules may affect the preparation process. Players should use Rockstar Support and the relevant platform's official instructions as the final reference.
