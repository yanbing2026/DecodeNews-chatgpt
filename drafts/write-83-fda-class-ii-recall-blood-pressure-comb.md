FDA将3,744瓶复方降压药召回列为Class II：两批次未通过溶出度测试

美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）将Macleods Pharma USA生产的一款复方降压药召回列为Class II。受影响药品为Olmesartan Medoxomil、Amlodipine和Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets，含三种降压成分。FDA资料显示，问题在于药片未达到溶出度规格，即在质量测试中未按要求溶解。此次召回涉及全国分销的3,744瓶药品，患者应先核对药瓶上的批号和National Drug Code（NDC），再联系药师或开药医生确认下一步处理方式。

## 召回涉及两个批次

FDA Enforcement Report记录显示，召回由Macleods Pharma USA主动发起，涉及两种瓶装规格：

- 30片装：3,528瓶，批号12251163A，NDC 33342-246-07。
- 90片装：216瓶，批号12251163B，NDC 33342-246-10。

两批药品的有效期均为2027年5月31日，产品规格为40 mg/10 mg/12.5 mg。FDA将该召回归为Class II，表示使用受影响产品可能导致暂时性或医学上可逆的不良健康后果，但出现严重后果的可能性被认为较低。该分类并不意味着每位服用者都会出现不良反应。

## 溶出度测试失败意味着什么

溶出度测试用于衡量药片在规定条件下释放有效成分的情况。药片未达到规定的溶出标准，意味着其释放药物成分的速度或程度不符合质量要求，可能影响药物在体内的释放和吸收。FDA公开的召回理由为“Failed Dissolution Specifications”，但目前公开资料没有说明具体哪一种成分或哪一项制造环节导致测试失败，也没有公布由此确认的患者伤害案例。

此次产品是三种成分组合的仿制药，分别为olmesartan medoxomil、amlodipine和hydrochlorothiazide。媒体将其称为Tribenzor的仿制版本；本次召回公告指向的是Macleods生产的指定批次仿制药，不应据此推断所有含相同成分的产品或品牌药均被召回。

## 召回时间与监管分类

Macleods于2026年10月7日启动自愿召回。FDA于10月26日将其列入执法报告并定为Class II。11月初，多家媒体再次报道这一召回，提醒消费者检查家中处方药瓶。FDA的官方记录是核对产品、批号和召回分类的首要来源；媒体报道可帮助理解召回范围，但不应取代FDA记录或药师的个别建议。

## 患者应核对药瓶并咨询专业人员

患者可查看处方瓶标签上的药品名称、NDC和批号，确认是否与上述两批次完全一致。若信息匹配，应联系配药药房或开药医生，询问替代药物和后续处理。Prevention援引FDA资料指出，FDA公开的该项召回记录没有提供具体面向消费者的停药指示。患者不应自行突然停用处方降压药；应先与医生或药师讨论，以避免血压控制中断。

如果药瓶信息不匹配，也不应仅凭药品成分相同就认定属于此次召回。此次召回的范围限定于指定生产商、药品规格、批号和NDC。患者或照护者如有疑问，应将药瓶带到药房核对，并关注FDA后续更新。

FDA Classifies Recall of 3,744 Bottles of Combination Blood Pressure Drug as Class II After Dissolution Test Failure

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has classified a recall involving a combination blood pressure medication made by Macleods Pharma USA as Class II. The affected product is Olmesartan Medoxomil, Amlodipine and Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets, which contains three blood-pressure-lowering ingredients. FDA records say the tablets failed dissolution specifications, meaning they did not dissolve as required during quality testing. The recall covers 3,744 bottles distributed nationwide. Patients should check the lot number and National Drug Code (NDC) on their prescription bottles and contact a pharmacist or prescriber to confirm what to do next.

## Two lots are included in the recall

The FDA Enforcement Report lists a voluntary recall initiated by Macleods Pharma USA. It covers two bottle sizes:

- 30-count bottles: 3,528 units, lot 12251163A, NDC 33342-246-07.
- 90-count bottles: 216 units, lot 12251163B, NDC 33342-246-10.

Both lots have an expiration date of May 31, 2027, and the product strength is 40 mg/10 mg/12.5 mg. The FDA classified the action as Class II. That category means use of the affected product may cause temporary or medically reversible adverse health consequences, while the likelihood of serious consequences is considered remote. The classification does not mean that every person who took the medication will experience an adverse effect.

## What a failed dissolution test means

Dissolution testing measures how a tablet releases its active ingredients under specified conditions. A tablet that does not meet the required dissolution specifications has not released its ingredients at the rate or extent required by quality standards. That could affect how the medicine is released and absorbed in the body. The FDA's stated reason for the recall is “Failed Dissolution Specifications.” Publicly available information does not identify which ingredient or manufacturing step caused the failure, and it does not report confirmed patient injuries resulting from the affected lots.

The product is a generic combination of olmesartan medoxomil, amlodipine and hydrochlorothiazide. Some media describe it as a generic version of Tribenzor. The recall notice identifies specific Macleods-manufactured generic lots; it should not be interpreted as a recall of every product containing the same ingredients or of all brand-name products.

## Recall timeline and regulatory classification

Macleods initiated the voluntary recall on October 7, 2026. The FDA added it to its Enforcement Report and classified it as Class II on October 26. In early November, multiple news organizations published reminders for consumers to check their prescription bottles. The FDA's official record is the primary reference for the affected product, lot numbers and recall classification. News coverage can help explain the scope, but it does not replace the FDA record or advice from a pharmacist who can review an individual prescription.

## Patients should check the label and contact a professional

Patients can compare the product name, NDC and lot number on their prescription bottle with the information above. If all details match, they should contact the dispensing pharmacy or prescribing clinician to discuss an alternative and next steps. Prevention, citing FDA information, reported that the FDA's public recall record did not provide specific consumer instructions to stop taking the medication. Patients should not abruptly discontinue prescribed blood pressure medicine on their own. They should first discuss the recall with a doctor or pharmacist to avoid an interruption in blood pressure treatment.

If the information on a bottle does not match, patients should not assume that it is included simply because the ingredients are the same. The recall is limited to the specified manufacturer, strength, lots and NDCs. Patients or caregivers with questions can take the bottle to a pharmacy for verification and monitor the FDA's recall information for updates.
