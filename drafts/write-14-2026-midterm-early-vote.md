# 2026年中期选举提前投票超过5700万，创纪录数字不代表选举结果

## 选举日前已有大量选民投票
NBC News的提前投票追踪器显示，截至11月2日晚上11时30分，全美已有57,243,189张邮寄及现场提前投票选票。统计包括提前现场投票和已寄回的邮寄选票。随着11月3日正式投票日到来，这一数字显示大量选民已经提前参与，但提前投票总量本身不能说明哪一党或哪位候选人领先。

## 现场与邮寄投票构成不同
NBC News追踪器显示，提前现场投票约为3,240万张，邮寄投票约为2,490万张。另有61,326,349张邮寄选票已被申请或寄出，但这一数字不等于已经投出的选票。部分州实行普遍邮寄投票，选票会自动寄给登记选民，因此“已寄出或申请”统计包含尚未寄回的选票。理解这两个数字的区别，有助于避免把选票分发量误当成实际参与人数。

## 与2022年中期选举比较
University of Florida Election Lab的统计显示，2022年中期选举最终记录的提前投票为45,920,446张。NBC News在2026年11月2日的统计已高于这一历史基准。不过，不同追踪机构使用的州级数据更新时间和处理方法可能不同，数字并不完全一致。提前投票总量也不能直接与最终投票率或选举结果画等号。

## 提前投票不等于候选人得票
提前投票追踪器统计的是已经投票的人数或选票数量，而不是候选人获得的票数。选票必须依照州法律，在规定时间内进行处理和计票。即使某个州的提前投票数量明显增加，也无法仅凭总量判断哪一方受益，因为不同候选人支持者的投票时间、邮寄投票习惯和选举日参与程度都可能不同。

## 最终结果仍需正式计票
提前投票数据可以帮助观察参与程度和投票方式，但部分州可能在选举日后继续接收符合规定的邮寄选票，计票和认证因此需要时间。对公众而言，最重要的区别是：提前投票数据是投票数量统计，不是候选人得票结果；最终结果必须以州和地方选举官员公布的正式计票为准。

# More Than 57 Million Votes Cast Early in 2026 Midterms, but the Total Is Not a Result

## Millions had voted before Election Day
NBC News’ early-vote tracker reported 57,243,189 mail-in and early in-person votes nationwide as of 11:30 p.m. Eastern on November 2. The total combines ballots returned by mail with votes cast in person before Election Day. With the general election scheduled for November 3, the figure shows that a large number of voters had already participated. It does not show which party or candidate is ahead, because early ballots are not candidate vote totals.

## Mail and in-person voting make up different shares
The NBC tracker showed approximately 32.4 million early in-person votes and 24.9 million mail votes. It separately listed 61,326,349 mail ballots as requested or sent. That second figure is not a count of ballots cast. Some states automatically send ballots to registered voters under universal vote-by-mail systems, so the total includes ballots that may not have been returned. The distinction matters when comparing participation with the number of ballots distributed.

## Comparison with the 2022 midterms
The University of Florida Election Lab recorded 45,920,446 early votes in the 2022 midterm elections. NBC News’ November 2, 2026, tally was already above that figure. However, national trackers do not always report identical totals. They receive state data on different schedules and may handle state reporting differences in different ways. Early-vote totals also should not be treated as a final turnout rate or as a prediction of the election outcome.

## Early-vote totals are not candidate results
Early-vote trackers count the number of people or ballots that have participated before Election Day. They do not report how those voters marked their ballots. Ballots must be processed and counted under state law and official procedures. Even a large increase in early voting in a particular state cannot by itself show which party benefits, because supporters of different candidates may have different voting schedules, mail-ballot habits and Election Day turnout.

## Official counting will determine the outcome
Early-vote data are useful for understanding participation and voting methods, but some states continue to receive eligible mail ballots after Election Day. Counting and certification can therefore take time. The central distinction is that early-vote trackers measure how many ballots have been cast, not who won. The final result must come from official counts released by state and local election officials.
