# 预测市场将民主党赢得参议院的概率推至74%，但赔率并非民调

## 选举前市场价格明显转向
美国中期选举投票日前一天，Kalshi交易者将民主党赢得参议院控制权的隐含概率推高至74%。CNBC报道，这是该平台自相关市场开放以来民主党获得的最高概率。推动市场变化的因素之一，是The New York Times与Siena University公布的摇摆州民调显示民主党候选人在多个关键州领先。不过，预测市场价格反映交易者对结果的判断，并不是对选民意向进行抽样调查。

## 关键州的市场赔率同步变化
CNBC报道，Kalshi上民主党赢得得州参议院席位的隐含概率升至74%，高于此前一天的68.4%。Quartz另报道，Polymarket对民主党赢得爱荷华州参议院选举的定价升至50%，此前为42%。堪萨斯州民主党候选人Adam Hamilton的市场胜率也上升。不同平台的合约和参与者结构不同，市场概率会随交易变化，不能把多个平台数字简单合并为一项民调。

## 民调与市场交易测量的不是同一件事
民调通过抽样询问选民来估计候选人支持率，预测市场则由交易者买卖与选举结果挂钩的合约。市场价格可能受到新闻、交易量、资金集中度和参与者风险偏好的影响。CNBC明确提醒，赔率平台没有采用传统民调的方法，因此不能替代民调。投票日前的市场价格也不能证明某个候选人已经领先或胜选。

## 参议院控制权仍取决于多州结果
民主党要取得参议院多数，需要在保住现有席位的同时赢得足够的共和党席位。多个州的选举结果将共同决定控制权，任何单一州的市场赔率都不能决定全国结果。The New York Times与Siena University的调查为交易者提供了新的信息，但民调本身也有抽样误差，且部分调查完成时间早于最后阶段的竞选变化。

## 市场概率应被视为一种信号
预测市场可以快速反映参与者对新消息的反应，但不等于确定的选举预测。Kalshi与Polymarket的交易价格可能在选举日当天继续变化，最终还要以州级选举官员的计票和认证结果为准。CNBC还披露其与Kalshi存在商业关系，包括客户获取合作和少数股权投资。读者在解读74%这一数字时，应同时考虑平台性质、媒体披露和选举尚未计票的事实。

# Prediction Markets Put Democrats’ Senate Chances at 74%, but Market Odds Are Not Polls

## Market prices shift toward Democrats before Election Day
On the eve of the 2026 midterm elections, traders on Kalshi priced the chance of Democrats winning control of the Senate at 74%. CNBC reported that this was the highest level for Democrats on the platform since the Senate-control market opened. One factor behind the move was a New York Times and Siena University polling report showing Democratic candidates leading in several battleground states. Prediction-market prices, however, reflect traders’ assessments of an outcome. They are not surveys of voter preferences.

## Odds also move in individual battleground races
CNBC reported that Kalshi traders raised the implied chance of a Democratic victory in the Texas Senate race to 74%, up from 68.4% over the preceding 24 hours. Quartz reported that Polymarket’s price for a Democratic win in Iowa rose to 50%, from 42%. The market price for Democrat Adam Hamilton in Kansas also increased. Different platforms have different contracts and participants, and prices move as people trade. Their figures should not be combined as if they were one poll.

## Polls and prediction markets measure different things
A poll uses a sample of voters to estimate candidate support. A prediction market lets participants buy and sell contracts tied to an election outcome. Prices can be affected by news, trading volume, concentrated positions and participants’ willingness to take risk. CNBC noted that odds platforms do not use the methods of traditional political polling and are not substitutes for polls. A market price before voting does not establish that a candidate is leading in actual ballots or has won.

## Senate control depends on results across several states
Democrats’ path to a Senate majority depends on retaining seats they currently hold while winning enough Republican-held seats. The outcome across multiple states will determine which party controls the chamber. No single state’s market odds can decide the national result. The New York Times and Siena University poll gave traders new information, but polls also have sampling error, and some surveys were conducted before the final campaign developments.

## Market probabilities should be treated as one signal
Prediction markets can react quickly to new information, but their prices are not certain forecasts of election results. Kalshi and Polymarket prices could continue changing on Election Day, and control of the Senate will ultimately be determined by official state vote counts and certification. CNBC also disclosed a commercial relationship with Kalshi that includes customer acquisition and a minority investment. Readers should consider the platform’s nature, the disclosure and the fact that votes had not yet been counted when interpreting the 74% figure.
