# 报道称Apple计划明年更新MacBook Pro，M7芯片与触控机型将成重点

## 新一代产品路线图曝光
Apple上周推出采用PROLED XDR显示屏的MacBook Pro后，下一步产品计划很快成为关注焦点。9to5Mac援引Bloomberg记者Mark Gurman的Power On报道指出，Apple计划在2027年推出搭载M7芯片的入门级MacBook Pro，并为高端触控机型推出第二代产品。Apple尚未公布这些产品的正式发布时间、规格或价格，因此相关信息仍属于媒体报道的产品路线预测。

## 入门机型或采用新机身设计
报道说，下一代基础款MacBook Pro预计采用近期高端机型使用的更薄、更轻机身和更窄边框设计。该机型将搭载标准版M7芯片，但现有信息并未确认它会配备OLED显示屏或触控功能。对Apple而言，将新设计下放至入门产品线，可能让不同价位机型在外观上更加接近，同时保留处理器和显示技术方面的产品区分。

## 高端触控机型预计继续升级
Gurman还预计，Apple会推出第二代触控MacBook Pro，使用M7 Pro和M7 Max芯片。此前报道曾称Apple可能跳过M6 Pro和M6 Max，直接转向M7系列，并将重点放在设备端AI处理能力提升上。不过，芯片的具体架构、性能指标和实际AI功能目前尚未公布，不能仅凭路线图推断产品性能提升幅度。

## Apple可能逐步整合高端产品线
报道还提出，Apple未来可能逐渐整合触控和非触控的高端MacBook Pro产品线。Gurman认为，同时维护两套高端产品会增加工程、营销和制造团队的负担，而两类机型价格差距有限，长期并行开发的必要性可能下降。这属于对公司产品策略的分析，并非Apple已确认会停产非触控机型。

## 发布节奏仍存在不确定性
当前报道预计标准版M7 MacBook Pro和第二代触控高端机型都将在2027年推出，但没有确定发布日期。Apple刚刚更新产品线，实际发布时间可能受到芯片开发、供应链和产品策略调整影响。消费者应将这些内容视为基于分析师报道的规划信息，而非已发布的产品承诺。

# Apple Reportedly Plans MacBook Pro Updates for 2027, With M7 Chips and Touch Models in Focus

## A reported roadmap points to another product refresh
After Apple introduced MacBook Pro models with a PROLED XDR display last week, attention quickly shifted to what may come next. 9to5Mac, citing Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman’s Power On newsletter, reported that Apple is planning a base MacBook Pro powered by an M7 chip for 2027, along with a second-generation high-end touchscreen model. Apple has not announced release dates, specifications or prices for these products, so the details remain reported plans rather than confirmed product commitments.

## The entry-level model may adopt a redesigned chassis
According to the report, the next base MacBook Pro is expected to use the thinner and lighter design and slimmer bezels introduced on the recently announced high-end models. It would use the standard M7 chip. The reporting does not confirm that the entry-level model will receive the PROLED XDR display or touchscreen features. Bringing the new exterior design to the base model could make the product family look more consistent while preserving distinctions in processors and display technology.

## High-end touchscreen models are expected to advance
Gurman also expects Apple to introduce a second-generation touchscreen MacBook Pro powered by M7 Pro and M7 Max chips. Earlier reporting said Apple may skip the M6 Pro and M6 Max generations and move directly to the M7 family, with a greater emphasis on on-device artificial-intelligence processing. Apple has not disclosed the chips’ architecture, performance figures or specific AI capabilities. The reported roadmap therefore does not establish how much faster the machines will be or what new software features they will enable.

## Apple may eventually consolidate its premium lineup
The report also raises the possibility that Apple could eventually bring its touch and non-touch high-end MacBook Pro lines together. Gurman has argued that maintaining two premium product families adds work for engineering, marketing and manufacturing teams, while the price difference between them may be small enough to make long-term separation less attractive. This is analysis about a possible product strategy, not confirmation that Apple has decided to discontinue non-touch models.

## Timing and specifications remain uncertain
The reported plan points to 2027 for both the base M7 MacBook Pro and the next high-end touchscreen models, but no specific launch date has been announced. Release timing could change as Apple develops the chips, manages its supply chain and adjusts its product plans. Buyers should treat the information as a report about Apple’s roadmap rather than an official announcement.
