# 监察长指控住房监管机构阻挠监督，Pulte与内部监察办公室冲突升级

## 监察长向国会报告阻挠问题
美国联邦住房金融局（FHFA）的监察长办公室指控，该机构在局长Bill Pulte领导下采取了“前所未有”的措施，妨碍监察长开展调查和监督。Bloomberg报道，这些指控写入监察长办公室提交国会的半年度报告，显示监管机构管理层与依法设立的内部监督部门之间冲突进一步升级。报告涉及FHFA对Fannie Mae、Freddie Mac及其他住房贷款机构的监管工作。

## 多项审查被指遭到拖延
监察长报告称，FHFA在至少三项评估中拒绝提供所要求的记录和信息，影响了对住房金融机构监管工作的审查。其中一项评估涉及FHFA如何监督一家多户住宅贷款机构；该贷款机构于2024年6月违约。报告称，虽然监察长办公室后来获得足以继续部分评估的资料，但此前的访问限制已造成重大延误，并可能影响报告按时完成。

## FHFA否认拒绝配合
FHFA发言人否认监察长对阻挠的描述，称机构已经提供资料并配合访谈。Reuters报道，FHFA表示，员工为三项近期审查完成了56项文件请求，并参加16场各约一小时的会议。双方对这些配合是否满足监察长依法取得资料的权限存在明显分歧。现有报道呈现的是监察长与机构的相互陈述，不能将监察长的指控直接写成法院已认定的违法行为。

## 监察长预算削减加剧冲突
这场争议还涉及监察长办公室预算。Bloomberg报道，Pulte此前宣布将大幅削减监察长办公室预算，削减幅度超过60%。Reuters称，监察长办公室工作人员被告知，裁员可能影响最多约80%的员工，约80人。监察长认为，预算削减将进一步削弱办公室执行法定监督职责的能力；FHFA则表示，监察长预算应接受财务管理和问责。

## 国会监督与机构独立性受考验
监察长办公室的职责是独立检查联邦机构的项目和管理，识别浪费、欺诈和管理不善。Reuters报道，这是该办公室至少十年来首次在半年度报告中指出FHFA干预其独立性。围绕Pulte的争议还包括他兼任FHFA局长及Fannie Mae、Freddie Mac董事会主席，以及政府问责局对其处理抵押贷款欺诈转介事项展开的另一项调查。相关调查仍在进行，不能视为已经认定个人违法。

# Watchdog Accuses Housing Regulator of Obstruction as Pulte Feud Escalates

## Inspector general reports alleged obstruction to Congress
The Office of Inspector General at the Federal Housing Finance Agency has accused the regulator, under Director Bill Pulte, of taking “unprecedented” steps that hindered the watchdog’s oversight work. Bloomberg reported that the allegations appeared in the inspector general’s semiannual report to Congress, marking a further escalation in the conflict between agency leadership and its congressionally mandated internal watchdog. The report concerns the FHFA’s oversight of Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and other housing lenders.

## Reviews were allegedly delayed by limits on access
The inspector general’s report says the FHFA refused to provide requested records and information during at least three separate evaluations. One review involved the agency’s oversight of a multifamily lender that defaulted in June 2024. The watchdog said that although it later received enough information to move some evaluations forward, the earlier restrictions had caused significant delays and could affect the office’s ability to complete reports for publication. The allegations are based on the inspector general’s account of its dealings with the agency.

## FHFA disputes the watchdog’s account
The FHFA rejected the characterization that it obstructed the inspector general. A spokesperson told Reuters that the agency had readily produced information and cooperated with the watchdog. The spokesperson said staff had completed 56 document requests and attended 16 separate meetings, each lasting about an hour, in connection with three recent reviews. The disagreement centers on whether those steps satisfied the inspector general’s statutory right to obtain records and conduct independent oversight. The reported allegations have not been adjudicated by a court.

## Budget cuts intensify the dispute
The dispute also concerns the inspector general’s funding. Bloomberg reported that Pulte had announced plans to cut the watchdog’s budget by more than 60%. Reuters reported that staff had been told the cuts could affect as much as 80% of the office’s workforce, or about 80 people. The inspector general warned that reduced funding would further impair its ability to carry out legally mandated oversight. The FHFA, in turn, has argued that the watchdog’s budget must remain subject to financial accountability and responsible management.

## Congressional oversight and independence are at stake
An inspector general is tasked with independently reviewing federal programs and identifying waste, fraud and mismanagement. Reuters reported that this was the first time in at least a decade that the FHFA watchdog had flagged interference with its independence in a semiannual report. The dispute comes amid broader scrutiny of Pulte, including his roles at the FHFA and on the boards of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, as well as a separate Government Accountability Office inquiry into his handling of mortgage-fraud referrals. Those matters remain under review; they should not be presented as findings that Pulte has committed wrongdoing.
