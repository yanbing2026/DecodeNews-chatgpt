# Google为《塞尔达传说：时之笛》推出彩蛋，可将搜索页面切换回1998年

Google 在《The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time》Switch 2 重制版推出前，加入了一项以游戏为主题的搜索彩蛋。用户搜索“Zelda”“Ocarina of Time”或“tloz”等关键词后，页面右下角会出现一把 Master Sword。点击图标后，搜索页面会变成类似1998年的旧版 Google 风格，以呼应这款任天堂经典游戏最初发行的年份。

## 搜索结果中出现大师之剑

Polygon 11月2日报道，Google 已在搜索结果中加入与《Ocarina of Time》相关的互动元素。用户搜索游戏名称或相关词语时，页面右下角会出现悬浮的 Master Sword 图案。点击后，剑会进入剑座，页面播放 Link 的标志性呼喊声，随后出现 Triforce 图案，搜索页面视觉风格也随之改变。

## 页面模拟1998年的搜索体验

彩蛋的主要效果是把 Google 搜索结果呈现为1998年的样式，也就是《Ocarina of Time》最初在 Nintendo 64 平台推出的年份。Nintendo Life 和 Video Games Chronicle 的报道也描述了相同互动：用户点击 Master Sword 后，页面切换到旧式搜索界面，展示简化的文字链接和早期 Google 标识。用户再次点击剑图标即可返回现代搜索页面。

## 与重制版发行时间相呼应

这一彩蛋出现的时间点与 Switch 2 版《Ocarina of Time》即将推出有关。Video Games Chronicle 报道称，重制版计划于11月5日发行。Google 此前也曾为电子游戏推出互动彩蛋，包括《Super Mario》主题的问号方块，以及与其他游戏相关的搜索页面效果。此次设计直接借用游戏中 Master Sword 与时间变化的意象。

## 互动效果并非完整复刻旧互联网

Google 的彩蛋主要改变搜索结果页面的视觉呈现，并不意味着用户能够浏览整个互联网的1998年版本。Video Games Chronicle 指出，旧版风格只作用于相关搜索页面，无法继续以这种方式浏览其他网站。它更像是一次短暂的视觉回顾，而不是对旧版搜索系统或互联网内容的完整恢复。

## 搜索产品与游戏文化的交集

这项功能显示，搜索平台仍会借助游戏发布节点推出轻量互动体验。对玩家而言，彩蛋提供了一个熟悉的游戏符号和怀旧界面；对 Google 而言，它也把搜索结果页变成了宣传文化事件的展示空间。报道没有说明该彩蛋会持续开放多久，也没有公布其使用量或是否会扩展到其他游戏。

目前能够确认的是，用户可以通过搜索相关游戏词语触发 Master Sword 动画，并将搜索页面切换为1998年风格。彩蛋的可用范围和持续时间仍未由 Google 单独说明。

# Google Adds an Ocarina of Time Easter Egg That Turns Search Results Back to 1998

Google has added an interactive search Easter egg inspired by The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time ahead of the game’s upcoming Nintendo Switch 2 remake. When users search for terms such as “Zelda,” “Ocarina of Time” or “tloz,” a Master Sword graphic appears in the lower-right corner of the results page. Clicking the sword triggers an animation and changes the page to resemble Google’s search results from 1998, the year the original Nintendo 64 game was released.

## The Master Sword appears in search results

Polygon reported on November 2 that Google had introduced the interactive feature as a tribute to the upcoming game release. Users can trigger it by searching for the game or related terms. A floating image of the Master Sword appears in the lower-right corner of the page. Clicking it places the sword into its pedestal, plays Link’s familiar shout and brings a Triforce graphic onto the screen.

The sequence uses imagery that will be familiar to players of Ocarina of Time. In the game, the Master Sword is central to the story’s time-travel mechanics. Google’s version turns that reference into a visual transition within the search interface.

## The page shifts to a 1998-style layout

After the animation, the search results take on the appearance of Google in 1998, when Ocarina of Time first appeared on the Nintendo 64. Nintendo Life and Video Games Chronicle described the same effect, including the old-fashioned text links and simplified search-page design. Users can click the sword again to return to the modern Google interface.

The effect is a visual recreation rather than a full restoration of the early web. Video Games Chronicle noted that the retro appearance is limited to the Zelda-related search page. It does not allow users to continue browsing the wider internet through a complete 1998 version of Google.

## The timing is tied to the remake’s release

The Easter egg arrives shortly before the Switch 2 remake of Ocarina of Time is scheduled to launch. Video Games Chronicle reported that the remake is due on November 5. The Google feature uses the game’s best-known visual symbols to mark the release and connect the modern search page with the year of the original title.

Google has created other game-related search interactions in the past. Polygon cited earlier examples including a Super Mario question block and other playful references to video games. These features turn ordinary search results into temporary interactive experiences tied to familiar entertainment properties.

## A short-lived interface tribute, not a complete time machine

The new feature changes the look of a particular search page, but it does not restore the old web or reproduce the full functionality of Google in 1998. The effect is designed as a brief tribute. Once users return to the standard interface, the modern search experience resumes.

Google has not announced how long the Easter egg will remain available. The reports also do not provide usage figures or say whether similar effects are planned for other upcoming games. Those details should not be inferred from the launch itself.

## Search products and gaming culture overlap

The feature illustrates how search platforms can use major game releases as opportunities for lightweight interactive design. For players, the Master Sword and retro interface provide a recognizable reference to a classic title. For Google, the search results page becomes a place to present a cultural event in a way that goes beyond a conventional text result.

The confirmed feature is straightforward: search for the game or related terms, click the Master Sword and watch the results page change to a 1998-inspired design. The duration and wider availability of the Easter egg remain unannounced.
