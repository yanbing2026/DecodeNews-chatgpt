# 《使命召唤：战区》将加入天气系统，Hajin地图迎来雨、雾与风暴变化

## 天气系统将随第一赛季上线
《使命召唤：战区》（Call of Duty: Warzone）将在《现代战争4》（Modern Warfare 4）第一赛季加入天气系统。Activision于11月2日公布第一赛季详情，官方确认新大逃杀地图Hajin将出现多种天气状况，包括晴朗、雾、风、降雨和远处风暴。第一赛季预计于11月6日上线，届时Warzone将与《现代战争4》全面整合。IGN将天气系统称为Warzone玩家长期期待的功能之一；GameSpot、Windows Central和其他游戏媒体也在新赛季报道中介绍了这项变化。

## 官方重点说明能见度仍是核心
Activision表示，Hajin的局部天气会改变环境的视觉效果和比赛氛围，但开发团队会确保能见度仍然是游戏体验的重要因素。新地图的两个Gulag竞技场版本也会受到当地天气影响。官方没有公布不同天气对武器、移动速度或伤害数值的具体影响，也没有说明所有天气状态是否会在比赛进行中动态切换。因此，目前可以确认的是天气变化会出现在Hajin的部署和战斗环境中，而不能把每一种可能的玩法影响都当成已经公布的机制。

## 天气是否在开局前固定仍有细节待确认
IGN的报道认为，天气似乎会在玩家进入比赛前确定，而不是像开放世界游戏那样在对局中从晴天逐渐转为暴雨。GamersExtra也把新系统描述为赛前选择天气条件。不过，Activision的官方公告只说明Hajin会出现多变的天气场景，没有明确说明天气是在匹配前固定、还是会在比赛过程中变化。玩家实际进入游戏后，才能确认天气变化的触发方式和具体节奏。雨、雾和风暴可能改变视野与环境音效，但这些影响的程度仍需等待完整更新说明和实战验证。

## 新地图与整合更新同步推出
天气系统是第一赛季整体更新的一部分。Hajin将作为大逃杀地图，背景设定横跨朝鲜半岛；另一张新地图Zodiac则用于Resurgence模式。Activision还公布了新的Zipline Launcher和Grabber Drone、可移动的燃料桶与掩体，以及新的进度挑战和武器强化机制。GamingTrend、Beebom、Dexerto和Dot Esports的报道均将天气系统放在新地图和第一赛季内容的整体变化中介绍。官方第一赛季资料同时确认，Warzone将在新赛季登陆Nintendo Switch 2。

## 这项变化能否影响战术仍要看实战
Warzone长期使用熟悉的地图环境，天气变化可能让同一地点在不同对局中呈现不同的视线和氛围。雾可能降低远距离观察能力，降雨和风暴也可能改变玩家对环境的判断；不过，Activision目前强调的是视觉环境和能见度，并未公布天气会如何改变枪械性能或脚步声判定。玩家也不应把媒体推测的赛前天气锁定机制当作最终规格。第一赛季上线后，天气在Hajin中的实际变化范围、对战斗公平性的影响，以及是否会成为稳定的战术变量，才有机会得到验证。

# Call of Duty: Warzone Is Getting a Weather System, Bringing Rain, Fog and Storms to Hajin

## Weather arrives with Season 1
Call of Duty: Warzone is set to receive a weather system as part of its integration with Modern Warfare 4 in Season 1. Activision published the season details on November 2 and confirmed that the new Battle Royale map, Hajin, will feature a range of local weather conditions, including clear skies, fog, wind, rain and distant storms. Season 1 is scheduled to launch on November 6, when Warzone will be fully integrated with Modern Warfare 4. IGN described weather as one of the features Warzone players have wanted for some time. GameSpot, Windows Central and other gaming outlets also included the change in their coverage of the upcoming season.

## Activision says visibility will remain central
Activision said localized weather patterns on Hajin will change the look and feel of the environment, while emphasizing that visibility will remain a central consideration in gameplay. The two versions of the new Hajin Gulag will also be exposed to local weather conditions. The official announcement did not specify how different weather states will affect weapon performance, movement speeds or damage values. It also did not explain whether every weather condition can change dynamically after a match begins. What is confirmed is that variable weather will be part of the deployment and combat environment on Hajin; every possible gameplay effect described by commentators should not be treated as an announced mechanic.

## The timing of weather changes is not fully clear
IGN suggested that weather may be determined before players load into a match, rather than shifting from clear skies to a storm during a live round as it might in an open-world game. GamersExtra also described the system as using pre-match weather conditions. Activision’s official announcement, however, says only that deployments to Hajin will feature variable weather scenarios. It does not explicitly say whether the conditions are locked before matchmaking or can change during a match. Players will need to experience the update to confirm how weather is selected and how it behaves during play. Rain, fog and storms could affect sightlines and the surrounding soundscape, but the scale of those effects remains to be tested.

## New maps and gameplay features arrive at the same time
The weather system is one part of a broader Season 1 update. Hajin will serve as the new Battle Royale map, with a setting spanning the Korean Peninsula. A second map, Zodiac, will be used for Resurgence. Activision has also announced new equipment, including the Zipline Launcher and Grabber Drone, movable fuel barrels and cover objects, and new progression and weapon-upgrade systems. GamingTrend, Beebom, Dexerto and Dot Esports all covered the weather feature as part of the wider map and Season 1 changes. The official Season 1 announcement also confirms that Warzone will launch on Nintendo Switch 2 with the update.

## The competitive impact will become clear after launch
Warzone has long relied on familiar map environments, so weather could make the same location look and feel different from one deployment to another. Fog may reduce long-range visibility, while rain and storms could change how players read the environment. Activision has so far emphasized environmental appearance and visibility, rather than announcing specific changes to weapon behavior or footstep detection. Players should also avoid treating media speculation about weather being locked before a match as a final technical specification. Once Season 1 launches, actual gameplay will show how much variation Hajin’s weather introduces, whether it affects combat balance and whether it becomes a meaningful tactical variable.
