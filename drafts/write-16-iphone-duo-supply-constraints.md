# Apple CEO Ternus称iPhone Duo供应仍受限，少量设备出现屏幕问题

## 供需何时平衡仍无法预测
Apple CEO John Ternus在接受Reuters采访时表示，iPhone Duo目前仍处于供应受限状态，公司正努力尽快向消费者交付产品，但暂时无法预测供需何时能够平衡。9to5Mac报道，Apple Store的预计发货时间仍排在明年1月下旬，这一等待时间自10月16日首轮预购迅速售罄后一直较长。Ternus没有给出具体的产能提升时间表。

## 屏幕问题涉及比例很小的设备
Ternus还回应了iPhone Duo内屏出现问题的报道。他表示，只有“非常小比例”的设备因显示屏问题被退回，Apple正在为这些用户更换新机。Apple此前已承认少量设备存在内屏问题，并建议受影响用户联系AppleCare。公司没有公布退货设备的具体数量，也没有公开确认问题的技术原因。

## 新机销售不在最新季度财报内
Apple公布的季度财报覆盖7月至9月期间，而iPhone Duo在该季度结束后才开始销售，因此财报没有包含该机型的销售收入。Apple当季收入为1,145亿美元，同比增长12%。Ternus的供应说明为投资者提供了新产品销售情况的线索，但公司并未披露iPhone Duo销量或积压订单总量。

## 供应限制与屏幕质量需要分别观察
目前公开信息显示，iPhone Duo面临供货不足，同时有少量设备被报告出现内屏问题。两者是不同的问题：供应受限涉及产能和交付节奏，显示屏退货涉及产品质量与售后处理。Apple称正在更换受影响设备，但尚未披露问题原因、维修方案细节或是否调整生产流程。

## 后续关注供货和官方说明
Apple尚未预测何时能够实现供需平衡，市场也无法仅凭发货估计判断实际销量。未来需要关注Apple Store交付时间是否缩短、公司是否公布更多产品销售数据，以及内屏问题是否出现进一步的官方说明。在这些信息出现之前，不能把少量退货扩大为普遍故障，也不能把较长交货时间直接等同于长期需求预测。

# Apple CEO John Ternus Says iPhone Duo Supply Remains Constrained, With a Small Number of Display Returns

## Apple cannot yet predict when supply will catch up
Apple CEO John Ternus said in an interview with Reuters that the iPhone Duo remains supply-constrained and that the company cannot yet predict when supply and demand will be in balance. 9to5Mac reported that Apple was working to get the device to customers as quickly as possible, but its online store continued to show delivery estimates in late January. Those estimates had remained extended since the first wave of preorders sold out on October 16. Ternus did not provide a timetable for a meaningful increase in supply.

## Display-related returns involve a small share of units
Ternus also addressed reports of problems with the iPhone Duo’s inner display. He said that a “very small percentage” of units had been returned because of display issues and that Apple was replacing those devices. Apple had previously acknowledged an issue affecting a small number of units and directed customers experiencing problems to AppleCare. The company has not disclosed the number of affected devices or publicly confirmed a technical cause.

## The device was not included in the latest quarterly results
Apple’s latest quarterly report covered the July-to-September period, while the iPhone Duo went on sale after the quarter ended. Its sales therefore were not included in the reported results. Apple reported quarterly revenue of $114.5 billion, up 12% year over year. Ternus’ comments give investors some insight into the launch, but Apple did not disclose iPhone Duo sales or the size of its order backlog.

## Supply constraints and display quality are separate issues
The available information points to two distinct issues: limited availability and a small number of reported display-related returns. Supply constraints concern production capacity and delivery schedules. Display returns concern product quality and after-sales support. Apple says it is replacing affected units, but it has not detailed the cause of the issue, the repair process or whether production procedures have changed.

## What to watch next
Apple has not provided a forecast for when supply and demand will balance, and shipping estimates alone cannot establish actual sales volume. Investors and customers will be watching whether delivery estimates improve, whether the company releases more information about product sales and whether Apple provides further technical details about the display issue. Until then, a small share of reported returns should not be described as a widespread failure, and long delivery estimates should not be treated as a complete measure of long-term demand.
