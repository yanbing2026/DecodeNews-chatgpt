# 得州州长选战预测市场收窄差距，Abbott仍被看好

## Abbott领先优势有所缩小
得克萨斯州州长选举成为2026年中期选举中备受关注的一场州级竞逐。共和党现任州长Greg Abbott寻求第四个任期，对手是民主党州众议员Gina Hinojosa。CNBC报道，选举前一天，预测市场仍认为Abbott更可能胜出，但Hinojosa的市场胜率在近几个月明显上升。预测市场反映交易者对结果的判断，并非传统民调，也不能直接代表选民投票意向。

## 两个平台显示民主党候选人升温
CNBC援引Kalshi数据称，Abbott在选举日获胜的隐含概率约为60%，Hinojosa约为40%。Hinojosa的胜率从7月中旬的8%升至11月2日的40%。Polymarket上的相关数字也显示，她的胜率从7月下旬低至5%上升至选举前一天的39%。这些数字会随交易变化，且不同平台的合约规则和参与者构成不同，不能视为科学抽样调查。

## 数据中心成为竞选争议焦点
这场选举还被视为得州选民对人工智能数据中心建设态度的一次观察。Abbott此前积极欢迎大型数据中心投资，曾宣传Google在得州的400亿美元投资计划，其中包括建设新的数据中心园区。但随着当地对电力需求、用水和基础设施压力的担忧上升，Abbott今年转向要求审查相关项目，并暂停部分许可审批。Hinojosa则主张暂缓新的数据中心项目，直到州议会制定监管保障措施。

## 早期投票规模已经可观
CNBC报道，得州现场提前投票于10月30日结束，已有超过770万名选民投票。这个数字说明选举日前已有大量选民完成投票，但不能仅凭提前投票总数推断哪一方占优。得州选民的党派结构、选区差异、邮寄选票和选举日投票仍会影响最终结果。

## 预测市场不能替代民调
预测市场参与者用资金交易选举结果合约，价格通常被解读为某种概率估计，但它不采用传统民调的抽样和加权方法。CNBC同时披露，该媒体与Kalshi存在商业关系，包括客户获取合作和少数股权投资。读者在解读相关数据时，应注意数据来源、市场流动性和媒体披露。选举结果最终由实际投票和官方计票决定，而非交易平台的赔率。

# Texas Governor Race Tightens in Prediction Markets, but Abbott Remains Favored

## Abbott’s lead has narrowed
The Texas governor’s race became one of the closely watched statewide contests in the 2026 midterm elections. Republican Gov. Greg Abbott is seeking a fourth term against Democratic state Rep. Gina Hinojosa. CNBC reported on the eve of Election Day that prediction-market traders still favored Abbott, but Hinojosa had made substantial gains in the markets over the preceding months. These prices reflect the views and trading activity of market participants. They are not traditional opinion polls and should not be read as a direct measure of voter preference.

## Two platforms show Hinojosa gaining ground
CNBC cited Kalshi contracts that implied roughly a 60% chance of an Abbott victory and a 40% chance for Hinojosa on November 2. Hinojosa’s implied chance on Kalshi had risen from 8% in mid-July to 40% on the eve of the election. On Polymarket, her odds had climbed from as low as 5% in late July to 39% on Monday. Those figures can move as contracts trade, and platforms may have different rules, participants and levels of liquidity. They are not equivalent to a representative survey of Texas voters.

## Data centers become a campaign issue
The race is also being watched as a possible test of public resistance to the rapid expansion of artificial-intelligence data centers. Abbott had previously promoted major technology investment in Texas. In November 2025, he touted a $40 billion Google investment plan that included three new data center campuses in the state. But concerns about electricity demand and the local effects of large facilities have grown. Abbott ordered an audit of data center projects seeking to connect to the state grid and later paused approvals for related permits while the state reviewed its existing queue. Hinojosa has called for an immediate moratorium on new data center projects until lawmakers establish stronger safeguards.

## Millions had already voted
CNBC reported that in-person early voting in Texas ended on October 30 and that more than 7.7 million Texans had already cast ballots. The early-vote total shows that a large share of the electorate had voted before Election Day, but it does not by itself indicate which candidate is ahead. Party composition, geographic turnout, mail ballots and votes cast on Election Day will all shape the final result.

## Prediction markets are not polling
Prediction-market contracts are traded by participants who put money behind possible outcomes. Their prices can be interpreted as market-implied probabilities, but the platforms do not use the sampling and weighting methods associated with traditional political polling. CNBC disclosed that it has a commercial relationship with Kalshi that includes customer acquisition and a minority investment. That disclosure is relevant when evaluating the report’s use of market data. Ultimately, the result will be determined by ballots cast and official counting, not by the odds displayed on trading platforms.
