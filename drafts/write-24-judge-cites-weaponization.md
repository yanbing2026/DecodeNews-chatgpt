# 法官以“武器化”批评驳回前FBI官员案件，司法部仍可寻求大陪审团起诉

## 法官驳回针对两名前FBI官员的刑事控告
美国华盛顿特区联邦治安法官Zia Faruqui于11月2日驳回针对两名前FBI官员Nikki Floris和Bradley Benavides的刑事控告。两人被指在国会听证中就Hunter Biden笔记本电脑调查及其自身参与情况作出虚假陈述。Associated Press报道，法官认为政府未能建立足够的合理根据，并在庭上严厉批评调查和起诉过程，称相关行动带有司法部被“武器化”的特征。

## 指控源于2023年国会证词
案件围绕两名前官员在2023年向众议院司法委员会作证时的陈述。检方指控他们对自己在FBI外国影响力工作组及Hunter Biden相关调查中的角色作出不实说明。NBC News报道，法官认为政府没有充分证明两人明知陈述虚假，或有意误导国会议员。法官还提到，调查启动和起诉时间靠近中期选举，引发对政治干预的担忧。

## 法官质疑调查过程与起诉时机
法官在听证中批评调查过程存在重大问题，并指出案件是在选举前不久提出，而相关证词发生在数年前。Associated Press报道，法官认为政府提交的材料未能达到提出刑事控告所需的合理根据标准。报道还提到，一名参与调查的FBI探员曾担心案件会被视为政治性调查。法官的言论是对本案调查和起诉方式的司法批评，不等于对所有相关政治争议作出普遍裁定。

## 司法部表示正在评估下一步
司法部表示不同意法官的决定，并称正在评估所有可用选项。由于案件仍可能进入后续程序，部门没有进一步评论。Associated Press和NBC News均指出，司法部理论上仍可尝试向大陪审团提交证据，寻求正式起诉。因此，驳回当前刑事控告并不必然意味着相关事项永远不能再被起诉，也不等同于陪审团审理后的无罪判决。

## 程序结果与事实认定必须区分
目前可以确认的是，法官驳回了针对两名前FBI官员的刑事控告，并认为现有材料没有达到合理根据门槛。两人并未在本案中被定罪。司法部是否会重新提交案件、能否提供新的证据，以及后续法院如何处理，仍未确定。对这类案件的报道，应将法官对程序的批评、政府的指控和最终是否构成犯罪分开呈现。

# Judge Cites “Weaponization” in Dismissing Case Against Former FBI Officials

## Judge dismisses criminal complaint against two former officials
A federal magistrate judge in Washington, D.C., on November 2 dismissed a criminal complaint against former FBI officials Nikki Floris and Bradley Benavides. The two were accused of making false statements to Congress about the FBI’s investigation involving Hunter Biden’s laptop and their own roles in related work. The Associated Press reported that Magistrate Judge Zia Faruqui found the government had not established sufficient probable cause and sharply criticized the investigation and prosecution, describing what he saw as the “weaponization” of the Justice Department.

## The allegations stem from 2023 congressional testimony
The case concerned statements the two former officials made during testimony before the House Judiciary Committee in 2023. Prosecutors alleged that they misrepresented their roles in the FBI’s Foreign Influence Task Force and in investigations involving Hunter Biden. NBC News reported that Faruqui found the government had not adequately shown that the officials knowingly made false statements or intended to mislead lawmakers. The judge also questioned the timing of the investigation and prosecution, which came close to the midterm elections.

## The judge questions the investigation and timing
During the hearing, Faruqui criticized what he viewed as serious problems in the investigation and noted that the case was brought shortly before an election even though the underlying testimony dated back several years. The Associated Press reported that the judge found the government’s materials did not meet the probable-cause standard required for the criminal complaint. The reporting also described testimony from an FBI agent involved in the investigation who had worried that the case would be perceived as political. The judge’s comments were criticisms of this investigation and prosecution, not a blanket ruling on every related political dispute.

## The Justice Department is evaluating its options
The Justice Department said it disagreed with the judge’s decision and was evaluating available options. The department declined to comment further while the matter remained an ongoing criminal investigation. The Associated Press and NBC News reported that prosecutors could theoretically seek to present evidence to a federal grand jury and pursue an indictment. The dismissal of the current complaint therefore does not necessarily prevent another prosecution, and it is not the same as an acquittal after a jury trial.

## The procedural outcome must be kept distinct from a final finding
The established result is that the judge dismissed the criminal complaint and found that the government had not met the probable-cause threshold on the materials presented. Neither former official was convicted in this case. Whether the Justice Department will pursue another route, whether it can produce additional evidence and how a later court would rule remain unknown. Coverage should distinguish the judge’s criticism of the process, the government’s allegations and any eventual determination of criminal liability.
