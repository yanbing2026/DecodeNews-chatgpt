# 美股11月2日收盘：科技股承压，Apple财报后盘后下跌

## 三大指数表现分化
美国股市11月2日走势反复，科技股抛售压力拖累纳斯达克指数，而道琼斯工业平均指数小幅上涨。Investopedia市场回顾显示，纳斯达克综合指数下跌1.1%，标普500指数下跌0.5%，道指上涨0.4%。投资者一方面关注人工智能相关股票估值和资本支出前景，另一方面等待大型科技公司财报，并观察中东局势变化对油价和市场风险偏好的影响。

## Apple财报略好于预期
Apple在收盘后公布季度业绩，收入略高于华尔街预期。The Wall Street Journal报道，公司称9月季度收入同比增长12%，毛利率为47.9%，两项数据略高于分析师预期。另一项市场统计显示，Apple季度收入为1,145亿美元，高于FactSet汇总的1,134亿美元预期。尽管如此，投资者仍关注服务业务增长放缓，以及涨价能否继续抵消成本压力。

## 财报电话会后股价走低
WSJ报道，Apple股价在财报电话会结束时盘后下跌约3%。市场关注点包括毛利率、服务业务表现和未来成本。Apple的iPhone Duo在9月季度结束后才开始销售，因此不计入这份季度财报。公司对新产品供需情况和未来成本的说明，将影响投资者对后续季度收入和利润率的判断。

## 油价大幅下跌提供部分支撑
当天油价下跌与市场对美国和伊朗可能通过外交方式缓和冲突的预期有关。Investopedia报道，WTI原油期货一度下跌约8%，至每桶95.80美元。能源价格回落有助于缓解部分通胀担忧，但并未阻止科技股在午后继续走弱。市场仍在权衡地缘政治风险、企业盈利和人工智能投资能否带来相应回报。

## AI股票估值仍是市场焦点
纳斯达克在10月经历明显回落后，投资者继续重新评估AI相关企业的估值和增长预期。Investopedia报道，标普500科技板块当天下跌2.7%，半导体股票普遍走弱。市场焦点不仅是单家公司的季度业绩，也包括高额数据中心和芯片投资能否转化为可持续现金流。短期股价波动并不能单独说明AI需求转弱，但说明市场对盈利兑现和估值的要求正在提高。

# U.S. Stocks on November 2: Tech Shares Weigh on Nasdaq as Apple Slips After Earnings

## Major indexes finish mixed
U.S. stocks traded unevenly on November 2 as pressure on technology shares weighed on the Nasdaq while the Dow Jones Industrial Average edged higher. Investopedia’s market recap reported that the Nasdaq Composite fell 1.1%, the S&P 500 declined 0.5%, and the Dow gained 0.4%. Investors were balancing concerns about valuations and spending by artificial-intelligence companies against expectations for major technology earnings and developments in the Middle East that could affect energy prices and market sentiment.

## Apple reports revenue slightly above expectations
Apple reported quarterly results after the market closed, with revenue narrowly exceeding Wall Street expectations. The Wall Street Journal said the company reported 12% year-over-year revenue growth for its September quarter and a gross margin of 47.9%, both slightly ahead of analyst estimates. A separate WSJ market update put revenue at $114.5 billion, compared with a FactSet analyst consensus of $113.4 billion. Investors nevertheless focused on slower growth in the high-margin services business and whether price increases could continue to protect margins.

## Shares fall after the earnings call
The Wall Street Journal reported that Apple shares were down about 3% in after-hours trading as the company’s earnings call wrapped up. Investors were assessing the margin outlook, services growth and cost pressures rather than revenue alone. Sales of the iPhone Duo were not included in the September-quarter results because the device went on sale after the quarter ended. Management’s comments about product supply and future costs therefore remain important to expectations for subsequent quarters.

## Lower oil prices offer a partial offset
Oil prices fell as investors considered signs that the United States and Iran might pursue diplomacy. Investopedia reported that West Texas Intermediate crude futures were down about 8% at $95.80 a barrel during the session. Lower energy prices can ease some inflation concerns, but they did not prevent technology shares from weakening later in the day. Markets continued to weigh geopolitical risks against corporate earnings and the potential return on large investments in artificial intelligence.

## AI valuations remain under pressure
After a difficult October for the Nasdaq, investors continued to reassess valuations and growth expectations for companies tied to artificial intelligence. Investopedia reported that the S&P 500 technology sector fell 2.7% on Monday and semiconductor shares broadly declined. The market debate is not limited to any single earnings report. It also concerns whether substantial spending on data centers and chips can translate into durable revenue and cash flow. A one-day decline does not by itself prove that AI demand is weakening, but it shows that investors are demanding clearer evidence that earnings can justify elevated valuations.
